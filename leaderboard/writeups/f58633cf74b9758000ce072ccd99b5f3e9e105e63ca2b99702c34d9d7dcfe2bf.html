Local-core output-stationary matmul

Local-core output-stationary matmul.

The idea

Each generated core owns a current B weight and a C accumulator. A rows and a shared next-weight feeder advance one matrix term per busy cycle, so the core performs its MAC beside its local weight.

This is a small model of a spatial wafer of independent cores and differs from the global operand shift reference by making the weight state per core. The design is parametric in N, W and AccW.

Why it scores as it does

As the record measured it, on sky130 (flow A0'): 91,311.3248 µm² × 4 cycles × 10,965 ps = 4,004,914.71 µm²·ns, 1.074 × par. In the ASAP7 view (the cells only): 1,387.57860 µm² × 4 cycles × 1,568 ps = 8,702.8930 µm²·ns, 1.059 × par.
