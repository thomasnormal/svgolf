# 4:2 compressor heap, two rows per leaf

A carry-save tree multiplier for every `N` in which each leaf holds two partial-product rows: `H = ⌈N/2⌉` leaves, each already a carry-save pair, every internal node a 4:2 compressor, and a ripple-carry adder on the root's two rows.

## The idea

Two rows of partial products already form a carry-save pair, so leaf `j` is `(S, C) = (pp[2j], pp[2j+1])`, both gather reads of a zero-padded copy of `a`. With the `H` leaves numbered as the last nodes of a heap (`H-1` to `2H-2`, node `k` with children `2k+1`, `2k+2`), every internal node compresses. Unlike the one-row-per-leaf heap, there are no pairing nodes and no constant selects to tell nodes apart.

The count is `localparam integer H = (N + 1) / 2`. The `sv_comb` tactics read it as one more constant of `N` and inline it as `(N + 1) / 2`, which `omega` handles. `b` is zero-extended to `2H+1` bits, so the last leaf of an odd `N` gets a zero row. Each compressor is two rows of full adders in the textbook form: three-input XOR sums and AND/OR majority carries.

## Why it scores as it does

At `N = 16` there are 8 leaves and 7 compressors in three levels. On sky130 it is about 8,563 µm² in 1,450 cells at 5,992 ps: about 51,311 µm²·ns, 0.57 × par. That is only a little better than the one-row-per-leaf heap (8,603 µm², 6,129 ps): that design's pairing nodes were already free after synthesis, so the localparam buys a simpler design and proof more than area. The critical path is 56 cells to `p[30]`, unit depth 62. On ASAP7: about 128.0 µm² at 1,224 ps, 156.6 µm²·ns. The same dataflow with re-chosen gate forms is a separate, faster record.

## The proof

307 lines on the tactic route. The arithmetic, `heap_mul` in `HeapArith`, is plain Lean. Each leaf is two gated, shifted lanes of `a` (`lane_gated`), and `csum_leaves2` sums the leaves to `a * b`. Each compressor keeps its children's value up to `2^(2N)` (`node_sum`, built from `csa_row` twice). The sum over the cut `{t, ..., 2t}` of the heap is then invariant modulo `2^(2N)` from `t = H-1` down to the root, by induction with no pairing case. The library's `ripple_row` finishes the final adder.

On the netlist side, `sv_structure` with the key `treeKey` (stage, node order, phase; `H` inlined) proves structure and acyclicity, `sv_interface` handles the ports and the localparam's valuation, sixteen `sv_bridge` lines turn the printed equations into `heap_mul`'s hypotheses, and `sv_cover` proves every heap read has a writer.
