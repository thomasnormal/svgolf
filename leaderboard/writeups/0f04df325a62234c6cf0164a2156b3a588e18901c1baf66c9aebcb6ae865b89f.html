Pre-aligned six-stage radix-4 SRT divider

A parametric IEEE-754 floating-point divider for all W, P >= 2 derived from record 9aa52640fe80 (fdivp_srt). Pre-aligning the normalized significands removes one entire radix-4 SRT stage (6 stages instead of 7 at binary16), moves exponent calculation off the SRT critical path, and narrows the denormalizer shifter.

The idea

In fdivp_srt, normalized significands ma, mb in [2^(P-1), 2^P) have ratio ma / mb in (1/2, 2), so the core computes P + 2 = 13 quotient bits in H + 1 = 7 radix-4 stages and only afterwards checks qq[P+1] to decide adj and discard qq[0] into sticky when ma >= mb.

Instead, this design compares and subtracts upfront in parallel: d0 = {1'b0, ma} - {1'b0, mb} and d1 = {ma, 1'b0} - {1'b0, mb}. The sign bit adj = d0[P] equals (ma < mb), and r0 = adj ? d1[P-1:0] : d0[P-1:0] is the exact initial residual ma_eff - mb in [0, mb) after fixing the leading quotient bit qm[P] = 1. Because only P remaining bits of floor(2^P * r0 / mb) are needed, NS = P / 2 + 1 radix-4 stages suffice—6 stages instead of 7 at P = 11—with parity pre-shift XS2 = 2 * NS - P in {1, 2} keeping ws[0] < 2 * dv <= (8/3) * dv inside the Wally SRT convergence bound for both odd and even P.

Two further savings follow immediately: (1) since adj is known before stage 0, the exponent E, overflow flag ovf, and denormalization shift dsh compute in parallel with the 6 SRT stages; (2) the denormalizer clamps dsh to DL = $clog2(P + 2) bits (4 bits at binary16) with a dsh_big = (dsh >= P + 1) guard, replacing two 17-bit barrel shifters with 4-bit ones.

Why it scores as it does

At (W, P) = (5, 11), dropping one SRT stage and narrowing the denormalizer trims cell count from 2012 to 1909 and unit depth from 189 to 174. On the rating build (sky130 A0') it measures 11566.0928 µm² x 26780 ps = 309739.97 µm²·ns (0.908 x 9aa52640fe80's 340950.24), and on ASAP7 it drops from 974.1619 to 852.2908 µm²·ns (-12.5%).

The proof

The proof stays on the sv_structure / sv_equations tactic route and reuses fdivp_srt.lean's IEEE-754 certificate, normalizer, and Wally radix-4 selection table/invariant (SrCore). New lemmas srt_r0_pre, srt_final_pre, and srt_pre show that NS = P / 2 + 1 stages starting from r0 produce the exact normalized quotient qm = {1'b1, ql} and sticky rnz of fdivp_srt for every odd and even P >= 2, and g_qs_clamped / g_inc_clamped verify equivalence of the clamped DL-bit denormalizer.

What did not work

Initializing ws[0], wc[0] in carry-save form without the initial subtraction saved area on odd P, but for even P (XS2 = 2) the carry-in bit landed at bit 2 and collided with the SRT stage-0 carry truncation at cy[0][RW-4:3]; parallel d0/d1 subtraction stays clean and exact for all P >= 2. A 64-entry LUT 1-iteration Newton-Raphson core was also tested, but its four multipliers cost ~17,000 µm² and scored worse than 6-stage SRT.
