# Restoring divider in about 90 plain assigns

This record is the restoring IEEE 754 divider of par, written so that every construct sits inside the fragment the `sv_comb` tactics read (`fdivp_d2.sv`, the design called D2 in the task notes). It is proved for every format `W >= 2`, `P >= 2`, and scored at binary16.

## The idea

D2 computes the same function by the same algorithm as par: `P - 1` one-bit normalizer stages per operand, `P + 2` restoring division steps, rounding to nearest even by an increment into the packed `{exponent, fraction}` word. What changes is the spelling. There is no `$clog2`, no `2**e`, no replication and no sized localparam; every count is affine in `P`. Three choices follow from that:

- the exponent is kept with an offset of `2^(W+P)` instead of in two's complement, and its threshold constants are built bit by bit as vectors;
- denormalization is a linear chain of `P + 1` one-bit right shifts that collects the sticky bit as it goes, instead of a run-time shifter;
- the output is a priority multiplexer over four precomputed words (NaN, infinity, zero, finite).

The result is about 90 plain assigns and five generate families (`norm`, `div`, `den`, `efg`, `nanz`).

## Why it scores as it does

On sky130 it is about 10,345 µm² at 38.3 ns, 396,148 µm²·ns, about 1.08 x par. The 1618 cells (par: 1515) include 449 `nand2` and 204 `mux2`. The serial restoring chain sets the delay in both designs (unit depth 295 here, 289 for par, and a critical path of 292 cells to `q[13]`), so the different spelling costs about 7% in area and 1% in delay. On the routed ASAP7 course it is 157.2 µm² at 7.13 ns, slightly faster than par's 7.25 ns, and 1120.7 µm²·ns, about 1.05 x par.

## The proof

The proof (4231 code lines) copies verbatim from par's proof everything that does not depend on the netlist: the facts about the trusted IEEE 754 spec, the rounding certificate `certF_bridge`, the `Nat` model `OutN` of the datapath with `OutN_fdivBits`, and a few shared glue lemmas. The new part is this netlist's glue. Section `D2Glue` has one lemma per shape of printed equation, a model `denIter` of the denormalizer with its invariant, and `exp_off` (the offset exponent is the true one plus `2^(W+P)`); `chain2` takes the 90 equations to the value `OutN` of `q`. The submission is `sv_structure` with a hand-written `rippleRank`, then `sv_interface`, `sv_equations` and `chain2`.

## What did not work

Many plain sites are expensive for the tactics: the task notes call their cost quadratic in the number of sites, and the proof raises its heartbeat limits to 4,000,000; the record lists a build time of about 115 s. The natural form D1 (with `$clog2`, `2**` and logarithmic shifters) is smaller but refused at load.
