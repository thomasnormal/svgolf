# Two-row-leaf 4:2 heap, late-input gate forms

The best entry of the task on both courses, sky130 and routed ASAP7, as of 2026-11-13. Its dataflow is exactly that of the two-row-leaf carry-save tree `mul_tree_h`: the same arrays, the same heap of 4:2 compressors, the same generate labels. What changed is how each cell is spelled.

## The idea

Synthesis keeps much of the structure it is given, so the Boolean form of a full adder moves both area and delay. Three changes, applied in every compressor row and in the final adder:

- XOR association: in each sum one chosen input is XORed last, for example `(S[2k+2] ^ C[2k+1]) ^ S[2k+1]` in a compressor's first row and `(X ^ C[2k+2]) ^ Y` in its second.
- Carries as multiplexers: the majority becomes `(p ^ q) ? r : q` (when two inputs differ the third decides), e.g. `(S[2k+2] ^ C[2k+1]) ? S[2k+1] : C[2k+1]`.
- In the final adder the ripple carry is the late signal: `p = S ^ (C ^ R)` and `R' = (C ^ S) ? R : S`, so the carry only passes through a mux data input per column.

The sum-row leaf AND also swaps its operands. The forms were found by a search over these choices, each candidate weighed with the scoring flow.

## Why it scores as it does

Against `mul_tree_h`, the area on sky130 falls from about 8,563 to 7,961 µm² and the delay from 5,992 to 5,342 ps, so area × time drops from about 51,311 to 42,530 µm²·ns, 0.47 × par. Of its 1,431 cells, 854 are NAND2 and 403 XOR2; the critical path is 57 cells to `p[30]`, unit depth 60. It led the ASAP7 course too on that date: about 120.9 µm² at 1,146 ps, 138.5 µm²·ns.

## The proof

It is the `mul_tree_h` proof plus one lemma, 360 lines in all. That proof's arithmetic core, here renamed `heap_mul0`, proves the multiplier from textbook cell equations (three-input XORs, AND/OR majorities): the leaves sum to `a * b`, each 4:2 node keeps its children's value (`node_sum`), and the sum over a cut of the heap is invariant modulo `2^(2N)` from the leaves down to the root.

The new `heap_mul` takes the equations in this design's forms, as `sv_equations` prints them, and discharges `heap_mul0`'s cell hypotheses from them: the leaf AND by commutativity, every other cell by generalizing its three Booleans and splitting, `cases s1 <;> cases c1 <;> cases s2 <;> decide`. The rest is unchanged: `sv_structure` with the key `treeKey`, `sv_interface`, sixteen `sv_bridge` lines and `sv_cover`. Gate-level golf of this kind costs one lemma of eight-case checks.
