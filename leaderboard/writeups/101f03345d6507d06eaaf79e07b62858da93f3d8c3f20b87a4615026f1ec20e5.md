# Kogge-Stone prefix adder

A Kogge-Stone parallel-prefix adder built from gates in generate loops, one design for every `N`. Every carry comes out of a network of logarithmic depth over the bits' generate/propagate pairs, so the delay grows with `log N` rather than `N`. The cost is a prefix node at almost every position on every level.

## The idea

The design numbers its positions from `0` to `N`. Position 0 is the carry in (generate `cin`, propagate 0), and position `k + 1` is bit `k`. There are `L = $clog2(N + 1)` levels, five at `N = 16`. At level `l`, every position `i` with `i >= 2^l` combines its pair with the pair `2^l` positions below it, `g | (p & g_low)` and `p & p_low`, and the lower positions pass through unchanged. After the last level, `g[L][k]` is the carry into bit `k`. The outputs are `s[i] = x[i] ^ g[L][i]` and `cout = g[L][N]`. All index arithmetic is on genvars and parameters.

## Why it scores as it does

It trades area for speed. On sky130 it takes 2064 ps over a 24-cell critical path, 21% faster than par's 2611 ps over 32 cells. The area is 641.9 µm² in 115 cells (51 NAND2, 30 AND2, 17 XOR2, 12 OR2, three XNOR2 and two NAND2B), 39% above par. So area times time is 1324.8 µm²·ns, about 10% over par. On the routed ASAP7 course it measures 418 ps and 9.48 µm², which gives 3.96 µm²·ns, close to par's 3.92.

## The proof

The `sv_comb` tactics refuse this source because of the conditional generate inside an inner generate loop and the `$clog2` localparam. The source was not rewritten to suit them. Instead the proof (1026 code lines) follows the by-hand route of the ripple reference:

1. `ks_instantiate2` instantiates the design at symbolic `N`. The localparam `L` is bound to the 32-bit truncation of `$clog2(N + 1)`, which equals `clog2 (N + 1)` within the task's range (`ksLv_eq`). Each node's generate-if expands through `GenItem.expand2_genIf_of_ne` and `_of_eq`.
2. `ksM_comb` shows that every process writes one bit and that the windows are pairwise disjoint (`ws_pairwise`). `ksM_acyclic` gives each pair of level `l` the rank `l + 1`.
3. The arithmetic uses plain `Bool` and `Nat`. `ksRec` is the recurrence. The invariant `ksRec_block` says that after level `l`, position `k` holds the pair of the block `[k + 1 - 2^l, k]`. Because the subtraction is on naturals, low positions saturate at block start 0, which covers the pass-through case. `pgBlock_combine` from the prefix library does the inductive step, and `ks_carry` reads off the ripple carry once `k < 2^L`.
4. `ks_settled` lifts the recurrence to every window of `g` and `p` by induction on the level. The sum and carry out then follow from `BitVec.getLsbD_add_add_bool` and `BitVec.carry_width`.
