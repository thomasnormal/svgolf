Four multiply-accumulate steps per cycle, an 8-bit counter

Four multiply-accumulate steps per cell per cycle, as in record 65342538360f: start captures A and B into two registers with three spare (zero) entries and rows and clears C and a step counter k; in each busy cycle every cell (i, j) adds A[i][k..k+3] . B[k..k+3][j] (sign-extended, wrapping at AccW; entries past N read as 0), A shifts by four entries, B by four rows and k grows by 4; done rises after ceil(N/4) busy cycles (latency (N+3)/4, 1 at the scoring point).

What it was built on

The design is that record's design with five lines changed: k is 8 bits wide instead of 32 (enough for N <= 256: while busy, k is a multiple of 4 below N), and the completion test k + 4 >= N is written (N <= 4) || (k >= N-4). At N = 4 that test is constant, so nothing reads the counter and synthesis removes it (770 flops against 800).

The design is parametric in N, W and AccW. The design is the player's file, byte for byte.

Why it scores as it does

As the record measured it, on sky130 (flow A0'): 239,698.6400 µm² × 1 cycle × 9,290 ps = 2,226,800.37 µm²·ns, 0.597 × par. In the ASAP7 view (the cells only): 3,627.89766 µm² × 1 cycle × 1,629 ps = 5,909.8453 µm²·ns, 0.719 × par.

The proof

The proof file has 1029 lines (798 of code, the board's count). 871 of them are the player's: its port of that record's first proof (the board's copy is the maintainers' later port of it) to this design, with the counter's width in the mirror and the queues, a lemma on the 8-bit test and the range N <= 256 carried to the busy step; 1054 of the 1114 lines of the player's file when it asked for help were lines of that proof, in its order.

Lines 2 to 4 are the header of record 65342538360f's proof, copied by the player; they describe that record's port, not this file.

The other 158 come from the answer a maintainers' helper, Claude Opus, gave to the player's help request (151 as handed over, 7 re-wrapped or edited by the player): the generated mirror's agreement (sv_mirror, mmG_D, mm_sem_eq) with the 32-bit range Rng32 threaded through the lemmas and the clock guard of termination, taken from record 65342538360f's proof (102 of the lines are lines of it), and the completion test as the instantiation writes it (gE, irreducible), its evaluation (eval_gE, gV, gV_known) and their uses.

Writeup by the maintainers, from the record's notes (their sentences, in the sections of a writeup) and the numbers the record and the ASAP7 view measured.
