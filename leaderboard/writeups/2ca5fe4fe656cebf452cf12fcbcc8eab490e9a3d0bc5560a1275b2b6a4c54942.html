Schoolbook multiply-accumulate; Strassen only at N = 2

At the scored size, N = 4, this design is a schoolbook multiply-accumulate schedule, the structure of par's design; its seven-product (Strassen) tile applies only at N = 2.

The idea

A 2 x 2 Strassen tile with a shift-register fallback, chosen by N at elaboration.

At every other N, the scoring point N = 4 included, the design is the fallback: an N x N array of multiply-accumulate cells fed by two shift registers that start loads with A and B, shifted by one word of A and one row of B per busy cycle, the structure of the reference design matmul_g1 (par), so its score there is close to par's.

At N = 2, start stores the seven Strassen products of the operands (sums and differences of entries of A and of B, sign-extended, wrapping at AccW), and in each of the two busy cycles C takes Strassen's four recombinations of them.

A 32-bit step counter k ends the busy phase after N steps and raises done (latency N). The design is parametric in N, W and AccW. The design is the player's file, byte for byte.

Why it scores as it does

As the record measured it, on sky130 (flow A0'): 88,060.7072 µm² × 4 cycles × 10,607 ps = 3,736,239.69 µm²·ns, 1.002 × par. In the ASAP7 view (the cells only): 1,344.26142 µm² × 4 cycles × 1,529 ps = 8,221.5028 µm²·ns, 1.001 × par.

The proof

The proof file has 1314 lines (1215 of code, the board's count). Ten of them are the player's: the header with the latency comment, the imports, the namespace and end, with their blank lines.

The other 1304 were written by Google Antigravity CLI with the model Gemini 4 Neon, working on its own as an autonomous agent, with the finished proofs of the records 1136031894d5, 5bff489cc2ef, 2b625ce36431, f14f8e008cc0 and ddc2d1b271db available as patterns (it shares the most lines with those of 1136031894d5 and 5bff489cc2ef, the same agent's earlier proofs): two mirrors of the design, one for N = 2 and one for every other N, each with its processes' disjointness, the clock cycle, the next state for each kind of cycle, the phase model, the interface, discipline and x-safety lemmas and termination; the Strassen recombination arithmetic; and the solution theorem joining the two cases. The maintainers wrote none of its lines.
