# Radix-4 Booth rows on a carry-save tape

An unsigned radix-4 (modified) Booth multiplier for every `N`. Recoding `b` into digits in `{-2, ..., 2}` cuts the partial-product rows to `K = (N + 3) / 2`, and a carry-save tape of full-adder rows reduces them before a ripple-carry adder.

## The idea

`b` is padded to `{0, b, 0}` and digit `k` is `-2·bb[2k+2] + bb[2k+1] + bb[2k]`; a small encoder per digit gives `one`, `two` and the sign. Row `k` selects `a`, `2a` or 0 on `N+1` bits and XORs it with the sign. A negative row's `+1` goes into a free column of the next row, and sign extension uses constant bits (`s, s, ~s` above row 0, `~s` and a one above the others) whose total vanishes modulo `2^(2N)`. Shifted reads go through padded gather vectors (`ae`, a window mask `wm`, a one-hot `pt`), so every index stays affine.

At `N = 16` that is 9 rows instead of 16. Seven carry-save adders, each reading the three oldest rows on the tape and appending a sum and a carry row, leave two rows for the final adder.

## Why it scores as it does

Fewer rows mean fewer adders: 1,345 cells, the fewest of the entries as of 2026-11-13, about 7,979 µm² on sky130. The encoder and row select add logic in front of the reduction, and the delay is 6,329 ps (63 cells to `p[31]`, unit depth 64), for about 50,498 µm²·ns, 0.56 × par. On ASAP7 it was the smallest design on 2026-11-13, about 120.3 µm², at 1,369 ps (164.7 µm²·ns), which puts it on that course's area-time frontier.

## The proof

768 lines of plain Lean over `CombLib`, connected to the netlist by `sv_structure` (with its own rank, `boothKey`), `sv_interface`, `sv_equations`, `sv_bridge` and `sv_cover`. The arithmetic:

1. A `bfVal` toolkit for rows built from windows and single bits: an OR of disjoint supports is a sum (`bfVal_or`), a complement is `2^n - 1 - x` (`bfVal_not`), a single bit is a power of two (`bfVal_pt`).
2. Each row's integer value by a case split on its three recoded bits (`rowK_int`, `row0_int`).
3. The Booth identity by induction on the rows (`booth_sum`): after `n` rows the sum is `a` times the recoded prefix of `b`, less the pending `+1`, plus the telescoped sign-extension constants.
4. A generic tape theorem (`tape_sum`), generalized from the Wallace reference: for any number of leaf rows, the last two rows sum to the leaves modulo `2^W`.

`booth_mul` puts them together.

## What did not work

Two sign-extension schemes were built, scored and simulated: the constants above, and a sign fill of the upper columns. The constants were kept.
