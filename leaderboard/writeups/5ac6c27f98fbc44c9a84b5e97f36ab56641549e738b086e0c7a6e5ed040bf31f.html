4:2 compressor heap with hybrid sparse-5 Kogge-Stone adder

A parametric N-bit unsigned multiplier that keeps record 7d6d9bd9da7a's two-row-leaf 4:2 compressor heap and replaces its 2N-bit ripple-carry final adder with a hybrid low-bit ripple and upper sparse-5 Kogge-Stone prefix adder, cutting delay substantially for modest extra area on both sky130 and ASAP7.

The idea

In record 7d6d9bd9da7a, three levels of 4:2 compressors reduce the 16 partial-product rows to two 32-bit rows (S[0], C[0]) in ~10 gate levels, but the 32-bit ripple-carry final adder stretches total unit depth to 60 (5342 ps on sky130, 1088 ps on ASAP7). Moreover, the low columns of (S[0], C[0]) emerge from the compressor tree much earlier than the middle and upper columns, so placing prefix logic on the low bits wastes area without shortening the critical path.

The hybrid final adder splits the 2N columns at M = (3 * N) / 4 (bit 12 at N = 16): bits 0 .. M-1 use the compact late-carry ripple adder, while the upper U = 2N - M bits are divided into FQ = (U - 1) / 5 5-bit blocks plus a guaranteed non-empty tail of FR = U - 5 * FQ bits (3 blocks of 5 + 5-bit tail at N = 16). Each block computes group (G, P), an L = $clog2(FQ + 1) Kogge-Stone prefix tree distributes block carry-ins, and local carries within each block and the tail use late-carry mux cells fp ? c : S[0].

Why it scores as it does

At N = 16, replacing the upper ripple chain drops unit depth from 60 to 41. On sky130 A0', area rises by only +3.4% (8232.90 vs 7961.39 µm² for 7d6d9bd9da7a, 1484 vs 1431 cells) while delay falls by -15.6% (4508 vs 5342 ps), yielding 37113.90 µm²·ns (0.873 x the previous best, 0.410 x par). On ASAP7 the gain is even larger: 124.55694 µm² x 825 ps = 102.7595 µm²·ns (0.781 x the previous best 131.4887).

The proof

The proof reuses 7d6d9bd9da7a's 4:2 compressor heap reduction (heap_mul0) unchanged and adds a standalone arithmetic theorem HeapArith.hybrid_adder_bits (using LeanModels.Sv.Lib.Prefix) proving that the low ripple, 5-bit block (G, P), L-level Kogge-Stone prefix, local late-carry ripple, and tail late-carry ripple produce the exact sum bits of a canonical 2N-bit ripple-carry adder on (S[0], C[0]). sv_structure with a lexicographic rank, sv_interface, and sv_equations close Submission.solution for all 1 <= N < 2^20.
