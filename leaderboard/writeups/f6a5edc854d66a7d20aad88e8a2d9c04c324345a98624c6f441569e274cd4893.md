# Weight-FIFO systolic array, cleared on start

`ws_fifo_array_clear.sv` is the same weight-FIFO systolic array as its sibling record. Its header cites the same published description of the TPU's matrix multiply unit (Jouppi et al., ISCA 2017). B enters through a FIFO into shadow weights, which shift down the array and are swapped into the active weights at step N. A enters as a diagonal wavefront through a triangle of delay stages, and partial sums move south. Each column ends in a rotating file of N accumulator words that share one adder. The latency is 4N, 16 cycles at N = 4.

## The idea

Two choices differ from the sibling. First, the start clears every register that matters: the data rows, the shadow and active weights, the partial sums and the accumulators. Second, the stored data and weights are W bits wide, and the sign extension to AccW bits happens at the multiplier inputs instead of when a value enters the array.

## Why it scores as it does

Both records have 1,650 flip-flops, but this one is larger and slower. It takes about 110,420 µm² on sky130 against 107,010, and its period is 10,402 ps against 8,229. Over 16 cycles that is about 18.4 million µm²·ns, 4.93 × par on sky130. On ASAP7 it is 4.75 × par, closer to the sibling's 4.55. The files do not separate how much comes from the clear multiplexers and how much from sign extension in the multiplier's path.

## The proof

This variant's interest is in the proof. Every register is cleared at the start, so every register family has an exact closed form t steps later. Next to `wfV`, `ubV`, `datV` and `cV`, the shadow weights, active weights and partial sums get `shV`, `waV` and `psV`, and each describes the whole register. The sibling needs partial-knowledge machinery: `PEnt` with its fold lemmas, and the invariants `shOK`, `waOK` and `psOK`. Here all of it is gone, replaced by start lemmas (`pe_start`, `mem_p0Q_start`, `mem_pkQ_start`) that show the clears. The proof is 2,317 code lines, against the sibling's 2,381.

The rest is shared:

- agreement with the hand-built `tpD` through the triangle offsets `base` and `DW` (`base_succ`, `base_N`);
- the clock step `tp_cycle` and commits register by register through `nbaFold`;
- the 32-bit counter tests, including `loB_tv` and `hiB_tv` for each column's accumulate window;
- the phase model, the three rules, and both of the statement's range bounds.
