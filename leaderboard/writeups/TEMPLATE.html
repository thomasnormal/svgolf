Short name of the idea (at most 60 characters)

One or two sentences: what the design is and how it compares with par and with the records before it. Copy this file to leaderboard/writeups/<record_hash>.html, the full hash of your record, and replace every part. It is an HTML fragment: <h2> sections, <p> paragraphs, lists, <code>, links and figures (README.md, "Writeups", lists what is allowed).

The idea

The circuit's architecture and its trick: what it computes where, and the trade-off between area and time it makes.

What it was built on

The record or the reference design it starts from, if any: its hash or file, linked, a diff of the two design files in a code block, and the licence notice kept if that file has one. A design of your own that only looks like another: say so, naming it. Leave this part out for a design that starts from nothing on the board.

Why it scores as it does

The numbers as your record measured them (the check compares every number with a unit of area or time with the measurements the tree holds): the area and what dominates it, the critical path or the clock period and the latency, the score and how it compares with par and with the records before it. A variant that is not a record, or a difference, as a ratio or a percentage.

The proof

How the proof goes: its structure, the invariant or the key lemmas, the tactics that did the work, what was hard, and the proof it was built on, if any.

What did not work

The variants that scored worse, the proofs that did not go through, and why. Leave this part out if there were none.
