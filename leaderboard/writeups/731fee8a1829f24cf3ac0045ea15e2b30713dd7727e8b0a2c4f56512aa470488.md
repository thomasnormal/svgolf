# 4:2 compressor heap, one row per leaf

A log-depth carry-save multiplier for every `N`. The `N` partial-product rows are the leaves of a binary tree numbered as a heap (node `k` has the children `2k+1` and `2k+2`), the internal nodes are 4:2 compressors, and the root's two rows go through a ripple-carry adder.

## The idea

The heap numbering replaces a stage counter. Every index is affine in `N` and the genvars, with no localparam, no division and no conditional generate, so the `sv_comb` tactics read the design as written. A leaf is one shifted row, `ae[c + 2N - 1 - j] & b[j]`, a gather read of a zero-padded copy `ae` of `a`, with a zero carry row. A node whose children are both leaves only pairs them into a carry-save pair; every other node is a 4:2 compressor, two stacked rows of full adders. Which is which is selected per node by constant vectors `th` and `nth` read at index `2k+1`, a constant select that synthesis removes. All sums are taken modulo `2^(2N)`, so the dropped top carries need no argument.

## Why it scores as it does

At `N = 16` the seven compressors form three levels. On sky130 the design is about 8,603 µm² in 1,456 cells at 6,129 ps, about 52,729 µm²·ns, 0.58 × par. The critical path is 58 cells to `p[30]` (unit depth 63), and the ripple adder at the root sets most of that depth. Constant-zero inputs, from the triangle of shifted rows and the leaves' zero carry rows, let synthesis shrink many full adders to half adders or wires. On ASAP7: about 128.6 µm² at 1,257 ps, 161.6 µm²·ns.

## The proof

322 lines on the tactic route. The arithmetic is plain Lean in `TreeArith`. `csa_row` shows a row of 3:2 counters keeps the column-weighted sum up to the dropped top carry, `node_sum` lifts that to a 4:2 node, `lane_gated` gives a leaf row's value, and `tree_mul` proves `p = a * b`. Its invariant is the sum over the cut `{t, ..., 2t}` of the heap: it stays the same modulo `2^(2N)` as `t` runs from `N-1` (the leaves, exactly `a * b`) down to 0, because node `t`'s children are exactly the two nodes the next cut adds. Pairing nodes keep the value exactly.

The netlist side is the tactics: `sv_structure` with the lexicographic key `treeKey` (stage, node order, phase) for structure and acyclicity, every goal `intros; sv_rowmajor; omega`; `sv_interface` for the ports; `sv_equations` with one `sv_bridge` per equation; and `sv_cover` for the heap reads. The column loops run over `2N`, which needed a larger generate-fuel bound in the tactic library, `4·N + 60` instead of `N + 60`.
