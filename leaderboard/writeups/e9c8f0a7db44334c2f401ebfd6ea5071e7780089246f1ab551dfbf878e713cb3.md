# Schoolbook with a balanced XOR tree per column

The top-ranked `clmul` record keeps par's `N^2` partial products but sums each output column with an explicit balanced XOR tree instead of chaining it through the rows. It is one design for every `N` (`clmul_tree.sv`), proved with the `sv_comb` tactics.

## The idea

The design is one 2-D packed array `T` of `2N - 1` columns by `2N - 1` positions, a tape per output column `k`:

- positions `0 .. N-1` are the column's partial products, zero-padded to exactly `N` leaves through padded copies of the operands (`ae`, `be`);
- XOR node `g`, for `g = 0 .. N-2`, writes position `N + g` from positions `2g` and `2g + 1`;
- the root is position `2N - 2`, and it drives `p[k]`.

This heap-order layout makes a perfect tree when `N` is a power of 2 and a valid tree for every other `N`, with one generate family for all columns. The padded leaves are constant zeros that synthesis folds away.

## Why it scores as it does

After synthesis this design and par are the same size: 481 cells with the identical mix (`xnor2` 225, `and2` 199, `nand2` 57), about 3,430 µm² on sky130. The difference is depth: the tree gives ABC a unit depth of 5 instead of 6, and the delay falls from 1.11 ns to 0.97 ns, a critical path of 5 cells to `p[13]`. That makes 3323.2 µm²·ns, about 0.87 x par and first on the board. The same holds on the routed ASAP7 course: 50.26 µm² at 159 ps, 7.99 µm²·ns, also first.

## The proof

The proof (231 code lines) keeps the shape of par's and replaces the row invariant with a window invariant:

- `wx f s n` is the XOR of the window `f s .. f (s + n - 1)` of a tape.
- `wx_window`: for every `g <= N - 1`, the live window `[2g, N + g)` XORs to the `N` leaves. Node `g` takes two positions off the front of the window and appends their XOR at the back, so one induction on `g` covers every `N`, not only powers of 2.
- `col_root` reads the root out of that invariant; `lower_leaves` and `upper_leaves` identify the leaves with the trusted statement's terms, the padding vanishing through `bx_trunc` and `bx_split`; `tree_spec` assembles all columns.

The structure step is `sv_structure` with the key `treeKey = (stage, node, 0)`: padded copies, leaves, then XOR nodes by `g`, then outputs. Ordering nodes by `g` is enough because the two positions node `g` reads are leaves or nodes numbered below `g`. Then `sv_equations`, eight `sv_bridge` facts into `tree_spec`, and `sv_cover` for the reads of the tape. The record lists a build time of about 15 s.
