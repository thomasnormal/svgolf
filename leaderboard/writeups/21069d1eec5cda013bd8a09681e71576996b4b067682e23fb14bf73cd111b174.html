Output cores with operand registers of their own

An array of output cores, each with operand registers of its own (its comment: a Cerebras-style wafer of small cores).

The idea

Start clears C and a 32-bit step counter k, captures A into a shift register and B, one row ahead, into a feeder register, and loads the two operand registers of every core (i, j) with A[i][0] and B[0][j]; in the first busy cycle every core adds the product of its two operand registers, in each later one the product of A[i][k], read from the shifted A register, and its B register, B[k][j] (both sign-extended, wrapping at AccW); in every busy cycle the operand registers take their next values from the A register and the feeder, A shifts by one entry and the feeder by one row.

After N steps the controller drops busy and raises done (latency N). Reset clears the operand, shift and feeder registers, C and the controller.

The design is parametric in N, W and AccW. The design is the player's file, byte for byte.

What it was built on

It is record 80aea08acec2's design (the same player's) with an A operand register in every core besides its B register, the A register's value used in the first step, and more registers cleared on reset; like it, it differs from the reference design matmul_g1 (par) in keeping per-core copies of operands.

Why it scores as it does

As the record measured it, on sky130 (flow A0'): 134,585.3280 µm² × 4 cycles × 10,052 ps = 5,411,406.87 µm²·ns, 1.451 × par. In the ASAP7 view (the cells only): 2,039.84406 µm² × 4 cycles × 1,581 ps = 12,899.9738 µm²·ns, 1.570 × par.

The proof

The proof file has 719 lines (654 of code, the board's count), all written by Google Antigravity CLI with the model Gemini 4 Neon, working on its own as an autonomous agent, in one commit (the player had no proof file for it), with the finished proofs of the records 80aea08acec2, 2b625ce36431, 5bff489cc2ef and 1136031894d5 available as patterns (it shares the most lines with that of 80aea08acec2, the same agent's earlier proof): the sv_mirror instantiation, the generated processes' disjointness and the clock cycle, the arithmetic and packing lemmas, the next state for each kind of cycle, the phase model, the interface, discipline and x-safety lemmas, termination and the solution theorem. The maintainers wrote none of its lines.
