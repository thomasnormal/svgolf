Registered 3D cube of partial sums

A registered 3D cube of partial sums: N layers of N x N accumulators, layer d for the d-th term of every dot product.

The idea

Start captures A and B into two operand registers, loads every cell (d, i, j) of the cube with the product A[i][d]*B[d][j] straight from the inputs (both sign-extended, wrapping at AccW), and clears C and a 32-bit step counter k; in each busy cycle every layer d >= 1 takes the cell of layer d-1 below it plus its own product, from the captured operands, so the partial sums move one layer deeper per cycle and after N - 1 cycles the last layer holds the whole dot products.

In the N-th busy cycle C takes the last layer, and the controller drops busy and raises done (latency N). It has N*N*N multipliers and N*N*N accumulators where the reference design matmul_g1 (par) has N*N of each.

The design is parametric in N, W and AccW. The design is the player's file, byte for byte.

Why it scores as it does

As the record measured it, on sky130 (flow A0'): 409,719.2032 µm² × 4 cycles × 10,953 ps = 17,950,617.73 µm²·ns, 4.813 × par. In the ASAP7 view (the cells only): 6,216.35796 µm² × 4 cycles × 1,557 ps = 38,715.4774 µm²·ns, 4.712 × par.

The proof

The proof file has 848 lines (773 of code, the board's count). Twelve of them are the player's: the header with the latency comment, the imports and the sv_mirror declaration.

The other 836 were written by Google Antigravity CLI with the model Gemini 4 Neon, working on its own as an autonomous agent, with the finished proofs of the records 2b625ce36431, f14f8e008cc0 and ddc2d1b271db available as patterns (it shares the most lines with that of 2b625ce36431, the same agent's earlier proof): the generated processes' disjointness and the clock cycle, the cube's invariant and the layer arithmetic, the next state for each kind of cycle, the phase model, the interface, discipline and x-safety lemmas, termination and the solution theorem. The maintainers wrote none of its lines.
