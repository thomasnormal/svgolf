Output memory bank, processing-in-memory style

An output memory bank in the processing-in-memory style (the player's description): C is a continuous copy of an N*N-word accumulator register, each word with its own multiply-accumulate.

The idea

Reset clears the words, the two operand banks and the controller; start clears the words and a 32-bit step counter k and captures A and B into the operand banks; in each busy cycle every word (i, j) adds A[i][k]*B[k][j], read at fixed places of the shifted banks (both sign-extended, wrapping at AccW), the A bank shifts by one entry and the B bank by one row, and busy and done are registered from k (done after the N-th step: latency N).

The design is parametric in N, W and AccW. The design is the player's file, byte for byte.

What it was built on

Its datapath is that of the reference design matmul_g1 (par): N*N multiply-accumulate cells fed by two shift registers; it differs in keeping the sums in a register of its own that drives C, in clearing it and the operand banks on reset, and in setting busy and done from k in every busy cycle.

Why it scores as it does

As the record measured it, on sky130 (flow A0'): 94,033.9360 µm² × 4 cycles × 10,768 ps = 4,050,229.69 µm²·ns, 1.086 × par. In the ASAP7 view (the cells only): 1,424.07234 µm² × 4 cycles × 1,521 ps = 8,664.0561 µm²·ns, 1.054 × par.

The proof

The proof file has 857 lines (785 of code, the board's count). 105 of them are the player's, kept from its earlier proof file, written for another design: the header with the latency comment, imports, blank lines and a few definitions and steps, among them the partial sums psum and accC (each of their longer lines is also a line of par's proof).

The other 752 were written by Google Antigravity CLI with the model Gemini 4 Neon, working on its own as an autonomous agent, with the finished proofs of the records 4d9952fba7ea, 1136031894d5, ddc2d1b271db and a248c56b42a9 available as patterns (it shares the most lines with that of 4d9952fba7ea, the same agent's earlier proof): the sv_mirror instantiation, the generated processes' disjointness and the clock cycle, the accumulator arithmetic, the next state for each kind of cycle, the phase model, the interface, discipline and x-safety lemmas, termination and the solution theorem. The maintainers wrote none of its lines.
