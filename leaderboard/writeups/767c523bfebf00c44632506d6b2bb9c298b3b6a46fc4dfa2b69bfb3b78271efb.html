Par's mask arbiter, the pick's AND operands swapped

A registered round-robin arbiter in the mask form: mask marks the clients above the last grant; the lowest masked requester wins, else the lowest requester (the wrap); gnt is a register, and the one combinational block computes the pick from req and mask.

What it was built on

The design is the reference design rr_mask (par's, record d354b82fb1ab) with one line changed: in the pick, the two ANDs have their operands swapped, (~mreq + 1'b1) & mreq for mreq & (~mreq + 1'b1) and likewise for req; every other byte, its comments included, is par's. The design is parametric in N. The design is the player's file, byte for byte.

Why it scores as it does

As the record measured it, on sky130 (flow A0'): 769.4880 µm² × 1 cycle × 2,826 ps = 2,174.57 µm²·ns, 1.000 × par. In the ASAP7 view (the cells only): 12.15972 µm² × 1 cycle × 464 ps = 5.6421 µm²·ns, 1.000 × par.

The proof

The proof file has 589 lines (430 of code, the board's count). It is par's proof rr_mask.lean, which the maintainers wrote with Claude, with five lines changed (the mirror's two pick expressions and the pick function's two lines, their operands swapped, and BitVec.and_comm added to one simp step) and four comment lines and one blank line of it left out: 584 of its lines are par's, in par's order. 556 lines are the player's, its file of 2026-11-11: par's proof as it was then, with those five lines changed. The other 33 were written by Google Antigravity CLI with the model Gemini 4 Neon, working on its own as an autonomous agent, in one commit that ported the file to the current checker (the sv_mirror instantiation and a 32-bit range hypothesis, as in par's current proof); each of them is a line of par's current proof.
