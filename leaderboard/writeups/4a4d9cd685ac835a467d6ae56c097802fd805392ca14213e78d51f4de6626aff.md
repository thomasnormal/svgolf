# Broadcast weight-stationary array, free-running PEs

`ws_broadcast.sv` computes the product on a weight-stationary array. Each of the N² processing elements (PEs) keeps one entry of B for the whole transaction, the rows of A stream past, and partial sums flow down the columns. The product takes 2N cycles, eight at the scoring point.

## The idea

On `start`, PE (k, j) latches B[k][j]. Column k of A feeds array row k through a delay line of k cycles. The head of each line is broadcast to every PE in its array row, not passed from PE to PE. Each PE multiplies that head by its weight and adds the partial sum of the PE above. From cycle N on, the bottom row of PEs holds a finished output row each cycle. That row enters the last row of `C` while the rows already collected move up one.

In this variant the PEs have no enable and compute in every cycle. A localparam `X` sign-extends the operands to max(AccW, W + 1) bits, so the multiply-accumulate is exactly AccW bits wide whenever W < AccW.

## Why it scores as it does

The array has the reference's N² multipliers and adders, plus weight, delay-line and partial-sum registers: 1,266 flip-flops and about 86,300 µm² on sky130, close to the reference's area. The period is 9,802 ps. The cost is the fill and drain: one product takes 2N cycles, twice the reference's N, and the score counts a single transaction. Area × time is about 6.77 million µm²·ns, 1.81 × par on sky130 and 1.85 × par on ASAP7. It is the better of the design's two records.

## The proof

The proof (1,484 code lines) follows the reference's order. `mm_sem_eq` matches the loaded design with a hand-built one: a control process and five generated families. This takes a disjointness argument for their write windows (`mm_gen_pairwise`). The localparam needs a case split on W < AccW (`xv`, `mm_paramEnv`).

The invariant `BusyAt` gives the control exactly and the rest window by window:

- `DlOk`: entry e of delay line k, t cycles in, is A[t + e − k][k] when e ≤ k ≤ t + e, and zero otherwise.
- `PsOk`: once t ≥ k + 1, PE (k, j) holds output row t − 1 − k, summed over its first k + 1 terms.
- `COk`: row i of `C` holds output row i + t − 2N once 2N ≤ i + t.

`next_busy` advances all three. `eq_ofBitVec_of_win` turns a vector known at every window into the packed vector, and `c_final` gives `C` = A·B at t = 2N. Because the PEs never stop, `next_idle` claims only that the control, the delay lines and `C` stay the same.
