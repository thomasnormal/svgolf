# Sklansky prefix adder

A Sklansky (divide-and-conquer) parallel-prefix adder in generate loops, proved for every `N`. Like the Kogge-Stone entry, it computes every carry in `$clog2(N + 1)` levels of generate/propagate combining. It needs fewer prefix nodes, though: on each level only the positions whose level bit is set do any work.

## The idea

Positions run from 0, the carry in with propagate 0, to `N`, where position `k + 1` is bit `k`. At level `l`, a position `i` whose bit `l` is set combines its pair with the top position of the block below it, `((i >> l) << l) - 1`. Every other position passes its pair through. After level `l`, each position holds the pair for the aligned block of positions that share its bits above `l`. So after the last level, `g[L][k]` is the carry into bit `k`. The cost is fan-out: the top of a block feeds every combining position in the block above it.

## Why it scores as it does

On sky130 its 95 cells (44 NAND2, 17 AND2, 16 XOR2, 9 OR2, 8 XNOR2 and one NOR2) take 541.8 µm², 18% above par. The delay is 2451 ps over a 27-cell critical path, 6% faster than par. Area times time is 1327.9 µm²·ns, about 10% over par and almost level with Kogge-Stone's 1324.8. The routed ASAP7 course ranks it differently: 8.05 µm² and 478 ps give 3.85 µm²·ns, ahead of par's design (3.92).

## The proof

The `sv_comb` tactics refuse this source: it has a conditional generate inside the level loop, a `$clog2` localparam, and a partner index that is not affine in the genvars. So the proof is written by hand, 1113 code lines on the route of the by-hand ripple reference:

1. **Instantiation** (`skl_instantiate2`). `L` is bound as a 32-bit `canonInt`, equal to `clog2 (N + 1)` below `2^31` (`lv_canon`). The generate-if guard evaluates to bit `l` of `i` (`guard_eval`), and the partner index to `i / 2^l * 2^l - 1` (`partE_eval`).
2. **Comb profile and acyclicity.** `skl_comb` shows the one-bit windows are pairwise disjoint (`pairwise_wins`). `skl_acyclic` gives a writer of `g` or `p` at flat offset `o` the rank `o + 1`. This works because row `l + 1` reads only row `l`, below its own offsets.
3. **The prefix mathematics.** A `Core` namespace, copied from the task's test suite, defines the recurrence `sklRec` and proves the invariant `sklRec_block`: after level `l`, position `i` holds `pgBlock` over `[(i >> l) << l, i]`. The inductive step splits on the guard. `aligned_odd` and `aligned_even` handle the arithmetic of aligned blocks, `block_op` merges two halves with `pgBlock_combine`, and `block_pass` covers the positions that pass through. `skl_carry_clog2` reads off the ripple carry at the last level.
4. **The settled state** (`skl_settled`) follows by induction on the level, and `CombTask.satisfiedAt_of_comb` gives the statement.
