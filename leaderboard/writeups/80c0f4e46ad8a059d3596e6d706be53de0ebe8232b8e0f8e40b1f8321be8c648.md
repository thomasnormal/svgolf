# Ripple carry, whole bit cell in one always_comb

This is the ripple-carry adder from the `always_comb` carry-cell entry, with the sum moved into the same block. Per bit, `p[i] = a[i] ^ b[i]` stays a continuous assign. One `always_comb` body sets `c[i+1]` (to `c[i]` when `p[i]` is set, otherwise to `a[i]`) and then `s[i] = p[i] ^ c[i]`. So a single procedural process writes two windows.

## The idea

The circuit is unchanged. The question is whether a block that drives two different signals still counts as a comb design to the checker and the tactics. The carry selection is the same multiplexer as before: when the operand bits agree, the carry out is their common value, and otherwise the carry in passes through.

## Why it scores as it does

Synthesis flattens the block into the same gates: 48 NAND2 and 32 XOR2 cells, 460.4 µm² and 2.61 ns over a 32-cell path to `s[15]`. That is 1202.2 µm²·ns on sky130, a tie with par. On the routed ASAP7 course it is 7.00 µm² and 558 ps, or 3.91 µm²·ns. That matches the one-window variant and is 2 ps faster than par's design.

## The proof

The proof (115 code lines) is the one-window variant's proof, character for character, and a test in the task suite checks that the two match. This works because of how the tactics name facts. `Design2.lowerComb` turns the block into one assign of the concatenation `{c[i+1], s[i]}`. `sv_equations` then states one equation and one knownness fact per *window*, named by the signal written and by that window's position among the windows writing the same signal. This design therefore has the aliases `eq_c_1`, `eq_p_1`, `eq_c_2`, `eq_s_1` and `eq_cout_1`, with exactly the statements the split design has, so a proof written against the aliases holds for both.

The rest of the proof matches too:

- `add_struct` runs `sv_structure` with a `rippleRank` that gives the cell its bit's rank, `i + 1`, whether it writes one window or two. Every obligation closes with `intros; omega`.
- `satisfiedAt_of_lowered` carries the lowered design's settled state back to `design.sem N`. `NoQ.sem_noQ` rules out qualified `case` statements with `decide`.
- `add_final` turns the equations into `CombLib.AddView` by a case split on three Booleans for the carry cell, and `add_spec` gives the sum and carry out.
