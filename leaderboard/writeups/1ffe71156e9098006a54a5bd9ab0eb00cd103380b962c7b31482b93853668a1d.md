# Four multiply-accumulate steps per cycle

`solution.sv` keeps the reference's layout, with one accumulator per entry of `C` and shift registers for the operands. Each unit, however, does four steps of the dot product per clock instead of one. At N = 4 that is the whole product in one cycle, and this record leads the matmul board.

## The idea

In each busy cycle, unit (i, j) adds A[i][k..k+3]·B[k..k+3][j] to its accumulator: four sign-extended products and the old value, summed in one expression. The step counter advances by four. `a_sh` shifts by four entries and `b_sh` by four rows. The captured operands are zero-padded so that the four windows stay static and in range for every N: `a_sh` has three spare entries and `b_sh` three spare rows. Steps past N therefore read zeros and add nothing. `done` rises after ceil(N/4) cycles, and the latency function is `(N + 3) / 4`. Both shifts are written as two shifts of half the amount, so that no shift amount can wrap at 32 bits anywhere in the parameter range.

## Why it scores as it does

Four times as many multipliers cost area: about 241,920 µm² on sky130, with about 38,700 cells and 800 flip-flops. That is 2.8 times the reference. The cycle count falls from 4 to 1, though, and the period does not grow. It is 9,410 ps over a 66-cell critical path, against the reference's 10,696 ps. Area × time comes to about 2.28 million µm²·ns, 0.61 × par, first on the sky130 board. On the routed ASAP7 course it is also first, at about 6,990 µm²·ns, 0.79 × the reference's 8,886.

## The proof

The proof (855 code lines) has the reference's structure: agreement with a hand-built design, the clock step, an invariant, the phase model and the three rules. The datapath facts are widened:

- `setWidth_add4_mul_signExtend`: four sign-extended multiply-accumulates at the wide width, truncated to AccW bits, equal the wrapped integer sum. `psum_add4` unrolls the partial sum by four steps.
- `padA`, `padB`, `winA_le`, `winB_le`: these define the padded operand registers and show that all eight windows lie inside them.
- `entry_B_zero`, `psum_ge`, `accC_ge`: a term whose row index is at least N reads B outside its width, hence zero, so overshooting N leaves the product unchanged.
- `ge_four_iff`, `toNat_mul3_lt`: the 32-bit counter test and the 32-bit shift amounts agree with their meaning in the integers.

The Busy phase counts busy cycles j, at step t = 4j, with j + m + 1 = (N + 3)/4.
