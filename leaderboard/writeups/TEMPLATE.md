# Short name of the idea (at most 60 characters)

One or two sentences: what the design is and what it scores (area, time, cycles, the score against par).
Copy this file to `leaderboard/writeups/<record_hash>.md`, the full hash of your record, and replace every part.

## The idea

The circuit's architecture and its trick: what it computes where, and the trade-off between area and time it makes.

## Why it scores as it does

The numbers, from your record or `score/ppa.py`: the area and what dominates it, the critical path or the clock
period and the latency, the score and how it compares with par and with the records before it.

## The proof

How the proof goes: its structure, the invariant or the key lemmas, the tactics that did the work, what was hard.

## What did not work

The variants that scored worse, the proofs that did not go through, and why. Leave this part out if there were none.
