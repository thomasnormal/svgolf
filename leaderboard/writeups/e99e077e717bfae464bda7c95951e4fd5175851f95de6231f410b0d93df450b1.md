# Multiplier cube with pipelined adder trees

`cube.sv` spends area to buy time. It forms all N³ products of the matrix product in one clock edge and then sums them in registered binary trees, so a 4 × 4 product is done three cycles after `start`.

## The idea

There is one product register per triple (i, j, k). The start edge writes A[i][k]·B[k][j] into it, read straight from the held ports with both entries sign-extended to AccW bits. For each output entry (i, j) these N registers are the leaves of a binary heap of 2N − 1 nodes stored in the flat vector `T`. Internal node x adds its children 2x + 1 and 2x + 2 on every clock, without an enable, and C copies the root on every clock as well. The only control is a step counter that raises `done` at clog2(N). No operand register shifts and no counter selects an operand. The latency is clog2(N) + 1.

## Why it scores as it does

On sky130 the design is 251,766 µm² with 2,754 flip-flops, 2.9 times the reference's 87,174 µm². It has 64 multipliers instead of 16, plus the tree registers. Each register stage holds one multiply or one adder level, never a multiply followed by an accumulate. So the period is 4,995 ps against the reference's 10,696 ps, and three cycles take 14,985 ps, about 0.35 of the reference's time. The two factors nearly cancel: about 3.77 million µm²·ns, 1.012 × par on sky130 and 1.157 × par on ASAP7.

## The proof

The proof has 1,098 code lines and uses `simp`, `omega`, `decide` and `grind`. `mm_sem_eq` identifies the loaded design with a hand-built `cbD` that has three nested generate loops. `mm_profile` and `mm_cycle` give the clock step, and `mmNext_regs` commits it register by register.

The arithmetic is kept apart from the hardware. `hv` gives a heap node's value: a leaf is its product, and an internal node is the sum of its children. `hv_span` replaces internal nodes by their children (`isum_children`) until only leaves remain, and `hv_root` concludes that the root is `Tasks.Matmul.dot`. The timing invariant `Good t` says that node y holds its value once N ≤ (y + 1)·2^t. `T_start` gives level 0, `T_step` adds one level per cycle, and `C_root` reads off the product once N ≤ 2^t, which holds at t = clog2(N). The phase model and the three rules (`mm_moore`, `mm_powerUp`, `mm_xsafe`) then feed the library's reduction to the statement.
