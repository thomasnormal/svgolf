# Spatial dataflow pipeline, one dot product per cycle

`spatial_pipeline.sv` does not copy the multiply-accumulate N² times. It lays the program "for each (i, j), fold over k" out as wired units: one compute unit with N multiplier lanes takes a new (i, j) item every cycle and delivers one entry of C per cycle. The layout is modelled on the Plasticine dataflow architecture of Prabhakar et al. (ISCA 2017).

## The idea

- A counter chain (`cc_i`, `cc_j`) issues the items row-major.
- Two scratchpads rotate so that their fixed read ports show row i of A and column j of B.
- The compute unit multiplies N sign-extended operand pairs into the leaves of a heap of 2P − 1 nodes, where P is N rounded up to a power of two and the spare leaves stay zero. A registered tree reduces them, one level per stage, with valid and last bits alongside.
- The output unit shifts each valid root into C, and the last item raises `done`.

N² items are in flight, and the latency is N² + clog2(N) + 2, which is 20 at N = 4.

## Why it scores as it does

On sky130 the design is 46,630 µm² with 1,044 flip-flops and four multipliers. That is about 54% of the reference's area and the smallest matmul design on the board as of 2026-11-13. No stage holds both a multiply and an add, so the period is 5,186 ps. But 20 cycles take 103,720 ps, 2.4 times the reference's time. The score is about 4.84 million µm²·ns: 1.297 × par on sky130 and 1.730 × par on ASAP7.

## The proof

The proof has 2,016 code lines. After agreement (`mm_sem_eq`) and the clock step (`mm_cycle`), its heart is the structure `BusyR`, with one field per register t cycles after a start. The scratchpads are the rotations `paV` and `pbV`. Bit s of the valid vector `vV` says that stage s is live (`vV_bit`). Tree node (d, x) holds `nodeV`, the sum of the products below it, for the item issued 1 + clog2(N) − d stages earlier, and the top of C holds the finished results in order. `busy_next` preserves the structure with one lemma per register (`step_pa`, `step_tr`, `step_c`, ...). `nodeV_root` turns the root into the dot product, with the zero lanes dropping out by `sumF_zero`. The proof also uses the task's second range bound, N·N·AccW below 2^31, because the shift of C by AccW takes a 32-bit amount.
