# Parametric Dadda tree, ripple final adder

A Dadda column-compression multiplier written once for every width. The stage count, the target heights and the full and half adders per column are closed forms in `N`, the stage and the column, so at `N = 16` the one file `dadda_rc.sv` builds the same adders as a per-width Dadda generator.

## The idea

Stage 0 holds the partial products by column: bit `i` of column `k` is `a[k-i-lo] & b[i+lo]`. Dadda's heights 2, 3, 4, 6, 9, 13, 19, ... (each the last plus half of it, rounded down) are the targets: stage `s` brings every column down to the next height, greedily, with full adders while a column is at least two bits over its target and then at most one half adder. At `N = 16` that is six stages (13, 9, 6, 4, 3, 2); a ripple-carry adder adds the last two rows.

The file uses generate constructs only, no functions. The height table is a chain of `?:` on the stage, the stage count `S` is a sum of comparisons against it (enough for the task's range, `N < 2^30`), and each count is computed inline and named by a one-iteration generate loop whose genvar equals it.

## Why it scores as it does

A column-wise schedule spends very few half adders, so the area is low: about 8,230 µm² on sky130 in 1,408 cells, at 5,461 ps, for about 44,946 µm²·ns, half of par (the array, 90,424). The critical path is 59 cells to `p[30]`, unit depth 61, and the ripple final adder sets most of that depth. On ASAP7 it is about 123.4 µm² at 1,187 ps, 146.5 µm²·ns; second on both courses.

## The proof

The `sv_comb` tactics do not read this shape (a stage count from `N`, inner loop bounds that mention the outer genvar, a conditional generate per column), so the 3,241-line proof is by hand, in four parts:

1. Arithmetic in plain Lean. The ledger `ldg`, the weighted sum of all column bits, is `a * b` on the partial products (`ldg_zero`) and is unchanged by a stage (`step_ledger`): a full adder is `x + y + z = s + 2c`, a half adder `x + y = s + 2c`. The adder counts obey linear identities (`id_carry`, `id_height`, ...), closed by one macro that splits the column index into regions for `omega`.
2. The design at symbolic `N`: the header expressions evaluate to the closed forms (`eT_eval`, `eF_eval`) and the generate nest expands to explicit cell lists (`gens_expand`).
3. Combinational profile and acyclicity: pairwise disjoint write windows (`dWs_pairwise`) and a rank by stage (`rkW`, `dProcs_reads`).
4. The settled state: induction over the stages (`cells_step`), then over the final adder's columns (`cells_K`), giving `dadda_settled`.
