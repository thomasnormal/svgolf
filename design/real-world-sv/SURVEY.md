# Real-world SystemVerilog vs the svGolf pipeline: construct census and gap ranking

Snapshot: lean-surfaces branch `svgolf-prep`, HEAD 55ef1eea (it moved from e862b83c while I worked; see limits), 2026-11-01. The refusal register (`docs/sv-refusals.json`) is a 2026-10-29 snapshot. Read-only: nothing under the repo was edited (`git status` clean at the end).

## 1. Method

1. **Counts.** I transcribed the `constructs` array of every surveyed entry (331 entries: clmul 30, arx 31, fpdiv 37, sbox 32, hashround 37, bitops 74, arith 57, alu 33; about 300 distinct files, because ibex_alu.sv, the OpenTitan `prim_*` files, cva6 multiplier and similar appear under several topics) into `$SCRATCH/survey_data.py` with synonyms merged (`function` + `automatic function` -> function (any); `part-select +:` and `-:` merged; `always @*` kept apart from `always_comb`). "Implementations" below always means: number of entries (of 331) listing the construct. Entries list what the surveyor noticed, so counts are lower bounds per file.
2. **Status by measurement, not by reading.** I wrote about 200 reduced probes, one per idiom, shaped as clmul, chacha-QR, S-box, adder and similar tasks, and ran the real checker `svgolf/check.py --unproved --tactics` on them (stages: source gate, extractor in sv-0.2 symbolic mode, banlist, fourstate, design load with `load_design_sv2`, then `sv_structure?`). 177 pipeline results are in `$SCRATCH/res/*.json`. Each probe was also run through `yosys -p "read_verilog -sv"` (Yosys 0.53). The check stops at the FIRST failing stage, so a message can hide later ones (a procedural loop reports the fourstate rule before the extractor's Unsupported node).
3. **Real-file cross-check.** The survey agents' downloaded files (`$SCRATCH/realworld/`) were fed to the extractor (`--top` = each module, last first): 507 module candidates, 440 compile, 213 are self-contained (no missing packages or modules); 163 of those 213 (77%) contain at least one `Unsupported` node (`$SCRATCH/realres.json`).
4. **Tactics caveat.** "In fragment" below means `sv_structure?` reads the design. `sv_equations` can still refuse (multi-window `always_comb` bodies: S1b gate d, in flight). I ran no proofs.
5. Register ids (`st-for`, `dc-net-z`, ...) are rows of `docs/sv-refusals.json`; `sg-*` ids are the svGolf-checker rows of the same file. Probe ids (p01, f01, c05, x40 ...) are files under `<scratchpad>/` (`probes/`, `cl/`, `q/`, `q/y/`, `q/g/`); the summary printers are `summ2.py`, `run_probe.py`, `probe_task.sh`, and the census is `realrun.py`.

## 2. Headline numbers

* **251 of 331 entries (76%)** use at least one construct that is refused before any proof can start (procedural loop, user function/task, `always @*`, casez/casex, unique/priority, explicit cast, block-local declaration, wire-with-initializer, macros/include/ifdef, x/z literal, interface/class/real/DPI, `$countones`, initial block, SVA). **155 (47%)** use a loop or a function. Of the other 80, 44 instantiate modules (the loader flattens them, the tactics refuse them). Only about 5 entries avoid everything we refuse or the tactics cannot read (coarse, vocabulary-level upper bound).
* **71 entries (21%)** use a construct Yosys 0.53 cannot read at all (`import` 62, `inside` 11, streaming 9, interface 4); those are out of scope for scoring regardless of ls-sv. `return` in functions is also unreadable (measured), but is not in the vocabulary.
* The biggest single surprise is not in the vocabulary: **every `wire` / implicit-net declaration is refused** (see gap 3), which is the whole Verilog-2001 style (about 79 entries are labelled Verilog).

## 3. Measured facts that shape the ranking

* **Loops die three times.** `for (int i...)` in `always_comb`: extractor emits `Unsupported ForLoopStatement` (f01, p01); the loop variable declaration is a `LocalDecl`, which the loader rejects for the whole envelope (f01); and the svGolf fourstate stage refuses `b[i]` with a signed 32-bit counter as a possible out-of-range read (`sg-fs-loop-index`, c05-c09, x20). `integer i` is `VariableSymbol:signed`, `^=` and `+=` are `AssignmentExpression:compound`.
* **`wire` is not available.** `wire [N-1:0] x;`, `input wire ...`, non-ANSI `input [7:0] a;`, scalar `wire t;` and `wire x = e;` all fail (v01-v14, s01-s03, g02, f17, x10): `assign target 'x' undeclared` or `decl 'a': unrecoverable packed dimensions`. `reg` and `logic` load. Even `wire logic [N-1:0]` fails. Registered as `dc-net-z` (later, NETRES), recorded in docs/sv-envelope-schema.md near line 2042, but it has no value_rank in the register's ranked list.
* **Constant part-selects on literal-width signals are "legacy part select" for the tactics** (x01, x07, x11, x24, x25, r01): `{d1[15:0], d1[31:16]}` in chacha_qr or `x[7:4]` in an S-box. The identical text on a parameter-width signal is accepted (s04-s07). A fixed-size task can dodge it by declaring signals through `localparam int W = 32` (x40 accepted). This is not in the `tl-sv-structure-fragment` trigger list.
* **Replication `{N{x}}` loads but the tactics refuse it** (f21, c04, x30, r18). The mask idiom `a & {N{b[i]}}` and zero-padding both hit it.
* **Self-determined positions.** `(a > b) ? a : b`, `if (a >= b)` and `c ? a : '0` are refused by the tactics (x28, w08, f38, g22, r17); writing the comparison into a one-bit signal first is accepted (r06). `a + b + ci`, `{1'b0,a} + {1'b0,b} + ci` and run-time `<<`/`>>` are accepted (r07, r08, f27; B2 gate 3a).
* **Source gate** (`svgolf/score/srcgate.py`, measured m1-m5, f04, x12, g21): rejects `` `define ``/`` `ifdef ``/`` `include ``, attributes, `unique`/`priority` (also `unique case`), `'z`/x digits outside casez/casex items; admits `` `default_nettype none|wire `` and casez wildcards.
* **Yosys 0.53 cannot read** (y-series, z-series, p-series): every `import` (module header, body, $unit), `return`, streaming `{<<{x}}`, `inside`, `localparam T [N] = '{..}` tables, `'{default:'0}`, `break`, `while` outside constant functions, `foreach`, `automatic` locals in `always_comb`, and a packed-struct port crashes it (assertion). It reads packages used as `pkg::name`, typedef/enum, functions without `return`, constant-bound loops, `$countones`, `unique case`.
* **What works today** (control designs): flat gate netlists over `logic` with bit selects; `assign` trees of `& | ^ ~ ! ?: == !=` and reductions; `~^` (f31); `a ^ (a >> 1)` (f32); generate bit-reversal (f34); 1-D/2-D packed arrays; two-deep generate-for with `g++`; `if/else` priority chains and default-then-override in `always_comb` (x22, k14, comb_ovr/prio_enc.sv); plain `case` with `default` and sized literal items (the 256-entry `sbox_table` reference); `+ - < <= > >=` at RHS level; run-time shifts; a fixed-size unpacked array with constant indices (f19, k10); `localparam integer` from `+ - *`/`/ %` by literals.

## 4. Ranked gap list (survey frequency x refusal x scorer readability)

1. **Procedural `for` in always_comb/functions** (112/331 = 34%, all 8 topics; clmul 18/30, hashround 20/37, bitops 29/74). Blockers: `st-for` (register rank 1), `ld-localdecl`, `st-compound`, `sg-fs-loop-index`. WPs: extractor + loader + fourstate + tactics. Suggest: desugar statically bounded loops that are accumulations (`acc = acc OP e(i)`, guarded by `if (b[i])`) or per-bit writes into the SSA/array chain the tactics already prove; replace the fourstate rule by an interval check on counters with static bounds.
2. **User functions (and tasks)** (function 96 = 29%, automatic 51, task 6). `ex-user-call` (rank 2). Inline in the extractor with fresh temporaries; needs the 4-state rule for unassigned locals (USERFN). Yosys cannot read `return`, so only name-assignment functions are scoreable.
3. **`wire` / implicit nets** (about 79 entries). `dc-net-z`. Cheapest high-reach fix: a `wire` with one continuous driver per bit is a `logic` plus assign; recover packed dims from syntax for implicit-type nets.
4. **Block-local and generate-local declarations** (66 = 20%). `ld-localdecl` (rank 3) and `GenerateDecl:Var`. Hoist to uniquely named module-level variables in the extractor.
5. **`always @*` / `always @(a or b)`** (60 = 18%; 25 of 213 real files). `pr-implicit-event`, `pr-event-list`. Accept as `always_comb` when the always_comb guards hold; no proof-layer change.
6. **Replication in the tactics** (104 = 31%). Add a replication constructor with affine count to the recognizer, shape theorem and knownness layer.
7. **Constant part-selects of literal-width signals** (part-select 114, concatenation 195; 12 of 31 ARX entries). Make the literal-bound case take the symbolic Select path (extractor) or add a `partSel` arm with affine bounds (CombView). Critical for fixed-size tasks (chacha_qr, aes_sbox, keccak).
8. **Module instances in the tactics** (141 = 43%; recursion 7, instance arrays 7). Loader flattens named, `.name`, `.*` and parameterised instances (r10, r11, k02, k03); `sv_structure` refuses all (WP-I step I0); recursion is `le-recursion` (r12). Karatsuba, Canright, Wallace trees, popcount trees need it.
9. **Explicit casts `N'(x)`** (49 = 15%; 19 real files). `cv-explicit` (rank 9). Lower width casts to Resize with symbolic size; today assign into a wider signal instead.
10. **Comparisons in conditions** (117 = 35% use comparisons). B2 gate 3c (`Part.cmp`) is the in-flight/unscheduled item. Dividers (`t >= d ? t - d : t`), max/min, sorting networks, FP special-case cascades all need it.
11. **Generate structure**: conditional generate inside loops or at top level (77), strides `i += 2` / `i = i*2` (`le-generate-genvar-step`, loader), loop start not a literal (w04), `N/2` packed bounds (g12-g14), `$clog2` in localparams and bounds (82, f22, f27), `%`/`*` indices (x36), 3-D packed (g15). Prefix adders (arith 21/57 use generate-if), barrel shifters, Keccak.
12. **`unique case` and `casez`** (39 = 12%, 13). `sg-qualifiers` (source gate), `st-cond-qual` (rank 7), `st-casez` (rank 6). casez lowers to masked-equality if-chains; unique with a default could lower to plain `case` (policy decision, WP5).
13. **Unpacked arrays** (102 = 31%). Parameter-sized arrays refused (f18, c01: `default-point-index/array`); rewrite to packed 2-D when indexed affinely (AGGR). ROM tables stay `case`.
14. **Signed** (`signed` 52, `$signed` 34, `>>>` 25). `dc-signed`: ports/vars refused (f14); `$signed()` loads but the tactics refuse `s<` and `s>>>` (g20, g11). fpdiv 20/37 and alu 16/33.
15. **Packages / typedef / enum / struct** (62 / 47 / 46 / 17). `import` is unreadable by Yosys 0.53 (cannot be scored at all); `pk::X` qualified refs are readable but ls-sv refuses package-scoped parameters in widths (k04), calls (k05), enum casts (k06, k07); typedef aliases fail (g17); packed struct variables fail (f12).
16. **Macros / include** (55 / 56): source gate (`sg-macro`, `sg-include`, plan MACROS). Low-medium; `sg-ifdef` is never.

**Re-ranking against the register's own value ranks** (`docs/sv-refusals.md` "Allowed later"): promote `always @*`, `wire`, replication, literal part-select, instances and conditional generate (none is in the ranked list); keep loops, functions, local declarations; demote `$countones` (rank 5, 1 entry), `inside` (rank 13, Yosys cannot read it), assignment patterns (rank 12, only unreadable ROM tables), compound assignment (rank 17) except as part of loops; 2-state `bit`/`int` (rank 10) loads for ports (f13) and was not seen in the survey vocabulary.

## 5. Places where docs and measurement differ (stale or missing rows)

* `ex-xnor` ("later"): `~^` extracts and is in the tactic fragment (f31).
* `dc-2state` ("later"): `bit` ports load (f13); the tactics refuse 2-state declarations.
* `wire` with packed range: no register row text names it (the message `unrecoverable packed dimensions` appears only in the tag of `ld-init-expr`, whose trigger is about initializers/typedefs).
* "legacy part select" and "replication" are tactic refusals; only replication is hinted in `tl-sv-structure-fragment`'s trigger list.
* `unique case` is refused by the svGolf source gate, not by ls-sv (the extractor accepts it, p07).
* The `ForLoopStatement` row (`st-for`) does not mention that the loop variable's `LocalDecl` and the fourstate loop-index rule each block independently.

## 6. Per-topic styles and blockers

* **clmul (30).** Shift-and-XOR accumulate loop (18 entries; `b[i] ? acc ^ (a << i) : acc`, `(a << i) & {2W{b[i]}}`, in always_comb, `always @*`, or a function); generate-for partial-product arrays with unpacked `pp[N]` and an XOR chain/tree (ibex, cogs); hand-unrolled 32-term flat XOR (VeeR, Arvern); triangular coefficient loops (cv32e40x); Karatsuba as per-width copies or recursive parameterised modules; Zbc variants by bit reversal + `>>1` or slicing one 2W product; GHASH/CRC relatives. Blockers: loops, `(2*N)'(a)` casts, unpacked `pp[N]`, `{N{b[i]}}` masks, functions, `always @*`, `wire`, conditional generate for boundary handling, recursion. In-fragment route: bit-level generate (`pp[i][j] = a[j] & b[i]`) on 2-D packed rows like the `mul_n` reference.
* **arx (31).** SSA temporaries (`a1`, `d2`) as an `assign` chain or in `always @*`/`always_comb`; rotation by concatenating constant part-selects (dominant) or `(x << n) | (x >> (W-n))` functions; QR as a module instantiated 4 or 8 times (17 entries); tasks/functions with in-place updates; microcoded or sequential wrappers (13 always_ff); Speck/Simon parameterised by word size. Blockers: constant part-selects on fixed-width signals (legacy part select), `always @*`, functions/tasks, instances (tactics), Verilog-2001 `wire`, sequential wrappers. Workaround: widths through `localparam W` (x40), `assign` chain with `+` (s07 accepted).
* **fpdiv (37).** Bit-serial restoring/non-restoring FSMs ({rem,quot} shift register; about half), SRT radix-4/16 with carry-save and ROM, combinational `/` `%` on shifted numerators (fdiv16 bans them), restoring loops in functions or always_comb, unrolled stage chains; FP wrapper: unpack/classify, special-case if-else cascade, casez or loop leading-zero count, signed exponent arithmetic, round GRS. Blockers: loops in functions, signed (20/37), casts, casez, comparisons in `if`/`?:` conditions (33 entries), generate-local `t1` stage variables (x31), unpacked stage arrays, packages (11), `$clog2`.
* **sbox (32).** 256-arm case LUT (accepted, `sbox_table`), unpacked constant tables or 256 `assign sbox[8'hNN]` lines, gate netlists (Boyar-Peralta 113/115 gates, Maximov, Calik) written as `wire t = a ^ b;` with `~^`, Canright as module trees (20 entries) or package functions, round glue (ShiftRows as `+:` wiring, xtime in five spellings, MixColumns loops), 4-bit ciphers via case functions. Blockers: `wire` with initializer, functions, instances (tactics), unique case/casez, `unpacked localparam` tables (Yosys cannot read), literal-width part-selects (fixed task), `import`.
* **hashround (37).** Keccak with a flat 1600-bit bus sliced `st[64*i +: 64]`, unpacked or packed-3-D lane arrays, `% 5` index arithmetic, rho/pi as tables; SHA/MD5 in secworks `always @*` style with named blocks and local regs, rotations by concatenated part-selects, Ch/Maj/Sigma as functions; CRC as Easics flat-XOR function, bit loop, 256-entry byte-LUT `unique case`, or elaboration-time mask generators. Blockers: functions (23), loops (20), unpacked arrays (20), computed `+:` bases, non-affine `%`/`*` indices, 3-D packed, `always @*` with local regs, literal-width part-selects.
* **bitops (74).** Popcount (accumulate loop, recursive halves, compressor LUT, `$countones`), clz/ctz (loop with break or found flag, generate trees, recursion, casez), priority encoders (loop, casez, if-chain, tree, ROM), barrel shifters (`{x,x} >> s`, log stages with `1 << i`, funnel via reversal), gray (`b ^ (b >> 1)` is in fragment), bit reverse (generate is in fragment), one-hot (OR masks, `1 << s` is in fragment), parity (`^x` in fragment), sorting networks (compare-swap on unpacked arrays). Blockers: loops (29), `$clog2` (40), comparison conditions, instances/recursion (22/5), conditional generate (20), casts (17), dynamic selects.
* **arith (57).** Prefix adders as generate level x bit with generate-if pass-through over `g[level][i]` arrays, or one always_comb with nested for; CLA/CSLA as hierarchical fixed-width modules; multipliers: `a & {N{b[i]}}` partial products, array rows of FA modules, Booth recoders, Wallace/Dadda mostly hard-wired per width, recursive compressor trees. Blockers: conditional generate in loops (21), unpacked arrays sized by LEVELS or N (26), `$clog2` localparams, instances (37), signed (13), replication (16).
* **alu (33).** lowRISC/PULP family (package enum opcode, `unique case`, one shared adder `{a,1'b1} + ({b,1'b0} ^ neg)`, left shift by bit reversal around one right shifter, comparator from adder/sign bits); tiny-core Verilog-2001 (macro opcodes, nested ternaries, one-hot flags, `$signed`/`>>>`); AND-OR masks with replication (VeeR, SERV); struct/enum/type parameters (cva6, cvw). Blockers: unique case (10), imports/enums/packed structs (16/8/7), signed (16), `>>>`, replication masks (23), macros/includes (8-9), `always @*` (8).

## 7. Limits

* **Survey side.** GitHub-heavy (code search rate-limited, no OpenCores/GitLab/Codeberg in most topics), popularity-biased, many vendored duplicates, a few LLM-assisted repos; no post-quantum GF(2)[x] multipliers or OpenCores Keccak found; BLAKE3 hardware thin. Construct lists are the surveyors' own readings, not a parser, so counts are lower bounds and my wire/Verilog count (about 79) is an estimate from the `language` fields. Some entries are non-synthesizable verification models (CESNET).
* **My side.** Probes are reductions of idioms, not the survey files themselves; verdicts are for those texts. `in_fragment` is `sv_structure?` only. The size sets behind "251 of 331" are vocabulary-level (a `SVA` or `INI` token may be benign in one file). The repository changed under me (HEAD e862b83c -> 55ef1eea: bv_decide reversal, M0-tier timed-loop/local-declaration commits); I re-ran 25 key probes at the new HEAD and all verdicts were identical. The real-file census is single-file compiles (missing packages inflate `Unsupported` kinds `UninstantiatedDef`/`Invalid*`, which I excluded). I did not run the full scoring flow, the postsynth stage, or any proof.
