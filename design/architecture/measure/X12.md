## The premise, corrected

The plan expected `execBody` to be a pure function, needing a monadic adapter. It is not pure. `execBody E : Stmt2 →
EM Unit` runs in the family monad `SemMWith Store Empty String Unit = ExceptT Empty (StateT Store (Except (Loud …)))`
(`LeanModels/Core/Outcome.lean`), and that stack already has a `WPMonad` instance with zero instances written (pinned
there by `example : WPMonad (SemMWith W ρ π σ) (SemPSWith W ρ π σ) := inferInstance`). `mvcgen` therefore runs on
`execBody` as it stands, so the stop rule's adapter case does not arise. Three one-time pieces were needed:

| piece | lines | heartbeats | why |
|---|---|---|---|
| `@[spec] ofRes_spec`: `ofRes` of a successful evaluation | 4 | 0.22 M | the evaluation's answer must be in the postcondition (the pilot's lesson) |
| `run_of_triple`: a total-correctness triple gives the run `x s0 = .ok (.ok a, s')` | 12 | 0.39 M | Std.Do's soundness lemmas (`WPSound`) cover `ExceptT` only over a `.pure`-shaped monad, not this three-layer stack; proved by unfolding `wp` (definitional at the `Except` layer) |
| `vw`: a whole-signal write is `SvState.set` on the view | 5 | — | the semantic fact every route needs (from L's `view_writeWin_decl`) |

## The comparison (MEASURED)

**Setup.** Every route proves the same statement, L's `DenotesE E I body g`: from every typed store satisfying `I`,
one `execBody` succeeds and ends in a typed store whose view is `g` of the view. The routes:
- **L**: compose L's primitives (`DenotesE.block/assignE/ite`, `DenotesLE.cons/nil`).
- **A**: A's one-step lemmas by hand (`execBody_assign_run`, `execBody_if_run`, `execBody_case_run`, `execArm_run`,
  `SemMWith.run_bind_ok`), with one 11-line helper `step_wr`.
- **mvcgen**: a `Std.Do` triple `⦃s = σ0⌝⦄ execBody E body ⦃⇓ _ s => ⌜Typed s ∧ view s = g (view σ0)⌝⦄`, closed by
  `mvcgen` and one `simp_all`, then a 3-line corollary through `run_of_triple`.

**The bodies:**
- B1 is G-B's fixture `mux1` (`begin t = a & b; if (c) y = t; else y = ~t; end`), verbatim.
- B2 is the asked-for loop-free `always_comb` with an `if/else` and two writes per branch.
- B3 (a five-statement chain ending in an `if`) and B4 (a `case` with two arms and a `default`) were added to see how
  each route scales and what it covers.

Heartbeats per body proof (`IO.getNumHeartbeats` around the command, `Elab.async false`, so kernel check included),
lines in brackets:

**Per-design boilerplate on the mvcgen route:** one `staticWin` fact per written signal (1 line) and one `vw`
instance per written signal (2 lines). Inside the proof, one `Elab.lookup_declared` per signal read or written.

**Where `mvcgen`'s cost goes** (diagnostic variants with the conditions left open, measured once; their output stays
in scratch): unfolding plus VC generation alone is 3.9 M on B1 and 4.1 M on B2, about half. The `simp_all` that closes
the VCs is the other half.

**What `mvcgen` leaves.** On B1 it leaves six conditions, all pure and exactly the semantic facts the other routes
need:
- four "the evaluation succeeds" (`∃ v, evalPure2 (view s) e = .ok v`) at the evolving store;
- one postcondition per branch.

The program walk, which L does by composition and A by a rewrite chain, is gone.

## Findings

1. **`mvcgen` works on `execBody` today, with no adapter** (MEASURED). On B4 it covers a construct L has no primitive
   for, with no new library: 12 lines, where A's hand route needs 27.
2. **It is 6 to 17 times more expensive than L's composition** (MEASURED: 7.9 M against 1.2 M on B1, 8.4 M against
   0.5 M on B2, 19.4 M against 3.0 M on B3). Lines are comparable to L (12-18 against 8-11); A's hand route is about
   twice as long.
3. **It scales with the store's nesting, not with the program** (MEASURED, B3). The VCs carry the store as nested
   `writeWin` terms. Each view rewrite needs `Typed` of the store under it, discharged once per enclosing write, so
   simp's default discharge depth (2) fails from the third write on (`maxDischargeDepth := 8` was needed). L's
   `DenotesE` hides the store behind the view function and has neither the cost nor the pitfall.
4. **Two pitfalls a user would hit** (MEASURED):
   - the orientation of the spec's postcondition (`.ok v = r`, not `r = .ok v`), or `simp_all` rewrites the value away
     from the `writeWin … (v.resizeU 4)` shape the view lemmas match;
   - a doc comment before a non-declaration command is a parse error (AGENTS.md's failure-mode table; hit twice).
5. **For scale** (X7, MEASURED there): today's route costs 14-20 M heartbeats per template site in total. One
   `always_comb` body proved through `mvcgen` costs 8-20 M on its own.

## Verdict (JUDGED)

**A niche tool, not a library.**
- Do not build the E proof route on `mvcgen`. L's `DenotesE` composition is an order of magnitude cheaper and as short,
  and its one gap measured here (`case`) is better closed by one more primitive. A's hand route for B4 (27 lines) is
  roughly what a `DenotesE.case` primitive would cost, once.
- Keep the three one-time pieces (`ofRes_spec`, `run_of_triple`, `vw`; 21 lines) available. `mvcgen` is then a
  zero-library fallback for a construct with no primitive yet, and a fast way to see a body's exact verification
  conditions. Here it produced them in one call, which made it the quickest way to learn what a primitive must state.
- If a library is ever wanted, the cost driver to fix first is the nested-store VC shape (item 3), for example a spec
  per statement kind stated on the view, not more `simp` power.
