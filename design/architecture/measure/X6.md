# X6: multi-point elaboration divergence

**Status label:** measurement. A Lean `#eval` batch (`probeReportString`, no theorem stated) plus pyslang. Agent M, WS4,
2026-11-02. Branch `arch-measure`, base `integ1102` at `d5fbdbc4`.

**Question (ARCH-NOTE §7 X6, §4.1, decision 9, §9 row "elaboration in the trusted definition is too big").** At parameter
points other than the default, how often does Lean's elaboration (`LeanModels/Sv/Param*.lean`, `Probe2.lean`) disagree
with slang's on widths, signedness, enum and constant values? Stop rule: if divergences are frequent, enumerate them and
keep the affected facts as declared annotations until Lean agrees.

**Reproduce.**
```
python3.12 -I x6_extract.py --repo . --census RB0_OUT --src RB0_SRC --out OUT -j 3   # points + extractor probes
python3 x6_lean.py --index OUT/index.json --lean OUT/X6Batch --max-mb 20               # 14 batch files
$SLOT lake env lean OUT/X6Batch_<k>.lean > OUT/lean_<k>.tsv                       # one slot run per batch
python3 x6_report.py --index OUT/index.json --tsv 'OUT/lean_*.tsv' --out x6.json
```
Inputs:
- RB0_OUT is the RB0 census output directory: `$SCRATCH/wprb0/out/final4`, the census's own envelopes.
- RB0_SRC is its fetch root.

The sources are third-party, sha256-checked against each envelope's `source_files`, and read under `python3.12 -I` by
[`run_extract.py`](run_extract.py). Data: [`x6.json`](x6.json), with the chosen points per design, every probe verdict
(`entries_compact`) and the divergences with their messages.

## Method (READ `Probe2.lean`, `x6_extract.py`)

The comparison is Probe2's multi-point cross-check, the mechanism ARCH-NOTE §4.1 names: "slang's results arrive only as
annotations, checked against Lean's at several parameter points". For each design point P:
1. `extract.py --probe-points` re-extracts the design with slang elaborating P itself.
2. Lean brings the default-point envelope to P (`PDesign.atPoint`, `instantiateG2`/`instantiateH`) and compares the two
   `Design2`s: declarations by `Decl` equality, which covers width, `isSigned`, `twoState` and initial value; processes
   structurally, which covers every literal, so folded enum and constant values too.
3. Lean also replays its elaboration at P against slang's `resolved` metadata at P: parameter values, localparams,
   declared widths, top-level generate counts.

Every instance of the hierarchy is probed at the overrides it receives at P. The verdicts are:

| verdict | meaning |
|---|---|
| `agree` | the two designs are equal and the metadata agrees |
| `differ` | a value differs: a silent default-point fold. Counted as a **divergence** |
| `metaDiffer` | Lean's elaboration disagrees with slang's metadata. Counted as a **divergence** |
| `unconfirmed` | equal except where one side is loud |
| `refused` | Lean cannot bring the envelope to P |
| `frontendRejects` | slang itself rejects P |

`parseError` means Lean's envelope parser rejects the design outright.

**Population (MEASURED).** 115 designs:
- 101 RB0 census envelopes (as-is and worked-around variants) whose top has an integer parameter;
- 14 svGolf references with a parameter (the 4 parameterless references are skipped).

**Points.** 876, at most 12 per design. For each integer parameter with default v, alone: v/2, v+1, 2v and 1. A 1-bit
parameter is flipped. With two or more integer parameters, one joint point doubles every one. For the references, the
task's own `probe_points` come first. Points a design does not accept are kept; slang's rejection is recorded.

## Results (MEASURED, `x6.json`)

**svGolf references: 113 of 113 points agree** (14 designs: band ×2, add ×5, clmul, fdivp, mul ×5).

**RB0 census blocks, 763 points:**

| outcome of the point | points | share |
|---|---|---|
| agrees (top and every instance) | 170 | 22 % |
| **diverges** | **6** | **0.8 %** |
| unconfirmed (one side loud) | 4 | 0.5 % |
| slang rejects the point | 36 | 5 % |
| Lean cannot bring the envelope to the point (`refused`) | 318 | 42 % |
| the envelope is not parsed by Lean at all (23 designs) | 229 | 30 % |

At the 180 points where both sides produce a design, 6 diverge (3.3 %). **All 6 are at points outside the design's
intended domain:**

| design (and variant) | point | what differs | why the point is degenerate |
|---|---|---|---|
| `serv_alu`, `serv_bufreg`, `serv_csr`, `serv_csr@wa2` | `W=2, B=0` | a context-width conversion: the envelope has `ident result_slt` where slang at the point has `resize 1 (ident result_slt)` (`serv_alu`), and likewise `clr_lsb` and `~d` | `B` defaults to `W-1`; the joint point breaks that, and `W=2` alone is `refused`. This is the default-point context-width fold of ARCH-NOTE §3.2: at the default every operand had the same width, so the extractor emitted no conversion. |
| `hazard3_priority_encode` (as-is and `@wa2`), instance `hazard3_onehot_encode#(W_REQ=1)` | `W_REQ=1` | declaration count 3 (envelope) vs 2 (point) | `W_GNT = $clog2(1) = 0`: a zero-width declaration; slang rejects the top at this point |

There are 0 `metaDiffer` verdicts: wherever both run, Lean's parameter values, localparams, declared widths and generate
counts equal slang's. The 4 `unconfirmed` verdicts are processes one side already refuses (an `always @*` R6 refusal in
`serv_decode`, an invalid `fcov` assign in `ibex_wb_stage`).

**The 17 census designs that Lean loads at the default point** (the RB0 census `LOADED` rows): 0 divergences and 0
refusals at their 81 other points. 79 entries agree; the rest are 1 `unconfirmed` and 11 `frontendRejects`
(`biriscv_issue@wa3`, `biriscv_regfile@wa3`).

**Where Lean cannot elaborate at P.** 378 entries are `refused`, by construct (MEASURED, base blocks affected in brackets):

| construct | entries | base blocks |
|---|---|---|
| replication `{N{…}}` in a parameter default (hazard3's config include) | 168 | 6 |
| an enum-typed parameter or localparam (`unknown enum type`) | 46 | 4 |
| an integer override of an enum-typed or untyped parameter (the probe tool's own limit) | 42 | 2 |
| `Constant:untyped-width` (an untyped parameter takes its value's type) | 27 | 4 |
| `unrecoverable packed dimensions` of an enum | 27 | 4 |
| a package-scoped parameter | 26 | 3 |
| concatenation in a parameter default | 24 | 1 |
| an unsupported node in a constant context (calls, scoped names) | 10 | 3 |
| unbased-unsized literal / unknown enum member | 8 | 2 |

The 23 unparsed envelopes carry statement or process kinds that the extractor emits and `Ingest2` does not parse:
`Initial` 18 (7 as a process, 11 inside a generate), `SysCall` 3, `LocalDecl` 2. All 23 were already refused by the RB0
census (`EXTRACTS-LOUD`), so this is not a regression on `integ1102`.

## Verdict

1. **Divergence is rare, and only at degenerate points.** Where Lean and slang both elaborate a point, they agree at 170
   of 180 census points (4 more are `unconfirmed`) and at all 113 reference points. The 6 divergences are at points outside the design's domain:
   a broken `B = W-1` relation, or a zero-width `$clog2(1)`. Probe2 catches each of them loudly. ARCH-NOTE's stop rule
   (frequent divergence → declared annotations) is **not** triggered.
2. **The risk to decision 9 is coverage, not disagreement.**
   - On real CPU blocks, Lean examines only 24 % of the probed points (180 of 763). It refuses 42 %: parameter defaults
     built from replication or concatenation, enum-typed parameters, untyped parameters, package-scoped parameters.
   - A further 30 % never reach the parameter question, because the envelope already holds constructs the Lean parser
     refuses.
   - The default-point check ("LOADED") selects exactly the designs that also elaborate everywhere else: 17 designs, 0
     refusals off the default.
3. **The one real fold class is context width.** The 4 serv divergences are the §3.2 caveat "context width is today
   taken from slang's default point", made visible. Probe2 catches the class at the probed points only. E's elaborator
   must compute context width per point (LRM 11.6–11.8), as §4.1 already requires.

## What this changes in ARCH-NOTE

- §9, row "elaboration in the trusted definition is too big": the measured risk is the elaborator's coverage, not
  disagreement (0 `metaDiffer`, 6 `differ` all at degenerate points, 113/113 on svGolf). The first R-ELAB work items are
  constant expressions with replication and concatenation (192 entries), enum-typed and untyped parameters (103), and
  package-scoped parameters (26). Together these are 85 % of the refusals (321 of 378).
- §3.2 "context width is taken from slang's default point": measured once, on serv. Keep Probe2's multi-point probes
  mandatory for svGolf; they are what caught it.
- Decision 9 (elaboration in Lean, slang a multi-point witness): confirmed as workable where Lean elaborates, with
  coverage the open item.
