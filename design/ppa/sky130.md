(`nor2b_1`, 1.667) costs the same as a plain AND2: a design whose legacy score leans on inverters and ANDNOT pays 1.2 to 1.5x on those gates. The
effect over the whole designs is a uniform-looking +9.5% to +18.3% (section 5.2) because the XOR/AND/MUX gates dominate.

### 2.4 Units of the power tables, and a trap

Liberty header: `time_unit 1ns`, `capacitive_load_unit (1.0, pf)`, `voltage_unit 1V`, `current_unit 1mA`, `leakage_power_unit 1nW`. So **pin capacitances are in pF** (WP-X0's
Nangate45 file used fF: any code with a hard-coded unit breaks) and `internal_power` values are V x mA x ns = **pJ**. The tables are *signed*: for `nand2_1` output Y
(mean over its power tables, 50 ps input slew):

| load fF | rise_power fJ | fall_power fJ | rise + fall fJ | 1/2 C V^2 fJ (1.8 V) |
|--:|--:|--:|--:|--:|
| 0.5 | 4.88 | 1.89 | 6.77 | 0.81 |
| 5 | 12.43 | -5.02 | 7.41 | 8.10 |
| 10 | 20.68 | -12.94 | 7.74 | 16.20 |
| 40 | 68.65 | -61.36 | 7.29 | 64.80 |
| 166.6 | 271.49 | -266.39 | 5.10 | 269.89 |

`rise_power` is about +1/2 C V^2 plus a load-independent part, `fall_power` about -1/2 C V^2 plus the same part: **the tables already contain the charge and discharge of the load**,
and only a rise followed by a fall leaves the cell's own energy (2 x about 3.7 fJ for a NAND2). WP-X0's `r * mean(rise tables) + f * mean(fall tables)` is correct for this library *because every net's
rises and falls alternate* (the pair-sum cancels the load term; the glitch-aware simulation keeps the alternation), while *adding* a separate "1/2 C V^2 per toggle" term (which WP-X0 and this study do, for the
net capacitance incl. the 10 fF output load) is the physical switching energy: its check is that rise + fall in the table is flat in the load (6.8 to 7.7 fJ from 0.5 to 40 fF). The input-pin power tables
(1-D) are ignored, as in WP-X0. Do not read a single `rise_power` entry as a per-toggle energy. Input-pin and output-pin tables with `when` conditions are averaged (the same simplification as WP-X0).

### 3.2 What the delay is, exactly

`liberty_weighted_delay_ps_sky130_v1` = the latest primary-output arrival of a **graph-based NLDM static timing analysis** (`engine.Design.sta_nldm`, 90 lines): arrival time and transition time per
(net, rising/falling); for each cell output and output edge, every timing arc of every related pin (each `when`-conditional arc as well; no false-path or state analysis) is evaluated by **bilinear interpolation
(linear extrapolation outside the table)** of `cell_rise`/`cell_fall` and `rise_transition`/`fall_transition` at (input transition, output load); the unateness of the arc selects the input edge; the worst arrival wins and
the largest output transition is carried per (net, edge) (OpenSTA's graph-based convention); load = sum of the fan-out input-pin capacitances + 10 fF per primary-output bit; inputs ideal. It is a cell-delay sum
along the critical path *with* load-dependent delays and slew propagation, not a unit-delay or logical-effort estimate. **It is a full NLDM STA of the combinational cone** but has been validated only against ABC's `stime -p` and not against OpenSTA; hence the conservative name.
Rounded to integer picoseconds. Deterministic: IEEE doubles with + - * / in a fixed order, no randomness (checked bit-identical across hash seeds, section 6.1).

**Validation against ABC's STA** (`spike/sky_validate.py`, measured): 98 mapped netlists (14 designs x 7 delay targets of `-D`, sized flow), engine T against ABC's `stime -p` Delay: **mean |error| 1.96%, maximum 6.7%** (mul_array, always +6.7%);
with the PIs driven by a `buf_1` through its tables (ABC's setup) 1.93% / 7.1%. WP-X0's Nangate45 figures were 1.3% / 5.5% on 72 netlists. The Yosys `stat -liberty` area equals the engine's area on every netlist (difference 0.0).

not a liberty cell of class `logic` (`policy_report`). All 84 mapped netlists of the study pass.

### 4.2 Constants and tie cells

The abstract-gate flow writes constants as literals (`assign y = {_0_, 2'h2, _1_}`) and prices them at 0. A real mapping flow adds tie cells: `hilomap -hicell sky130_fd_sc_hd__conb_1 HI -locell sky130_fd_sc_hd__conb_1 LO` after `abc`
creates **one `conb_1` per constant bit** (a 6-bit test output with four constant bits gave four cells, 25.0 um^2 in total); `hilomap -singleton` gives one HI and one LO cell per module (measured on `spike/tie_probe.sv`).
None of the 14 designs has a constant. **Measured failure of the obvious route**: a netlist with a `conb_1` instance connected on one output (what Yosys writes) is **rejected by the checker's strict Verilator lint** (`PINMISSING` on the unconnected `LO`/`HI`); the
upstream functional model of `conb` is made of `pullup`/`pulldown` primitives, on which Verilator reports `UNDRIVEN` (also fatal). **Proposed policy**: the scored netlist keeps constants as literals (postsynth unchanged, constants are known 0/1), and the
sky130 scorer prices them as **exactly one `conb_1` (3.7536 um^2, 1 cell, 1 GE) per module if any constant drives an output bit or a cell pin, none otherwise**, regardless of how many bits are constant. The abstract-gate flow's 0 is a loophole of the old flow; one tie cell is the smallest honest charge.

### 6.1 Run to run: bit-exact (measured)

### 6.4 The noise policy (proposal)

1. **Scores are deterministic; ties are about robustness, not about reruns.** Records are compared exactly (integers); the *board* additionally marks "within flow noise".
2. **Tie bands** from the tables above, one per metric, stated in the flow's pins: **area_um2 / cells: 10%** (design pairs further apart than that keep their order in 93.5% of the mapper-pair events, 360 of 385; the 10-20% bucket alone 92%; the commute noise of A0' area is <= 11.3% worst case, median 2.3%); **liberty-weighted delay: 20%** (beyond it 92% of the events keep the order, 544 of 590; the 10-20% bucket only 81%; A0' commute noise median 4.1%, worst 32%);
   **energy: 3%** (seed CV <= 1.5%, five-seed range <= 3.7%; of 528 pair-seed events with a gap above 3% none was reversed, in either flow, activity or glitch-aware; below 3% there were 6 reversals in 240); **legacy GE / depth: unchanged (exact)**.
3. **Rank intervals, not ranks, for the sky130 views.** For a metric m with band b: `rank_lo(i) = 1 + #{j : m_j < m_i (1 - b)}`, `rank_hi(i) = #{j : m_j <= m_i (1 + b)}`; show `lo..hi` and mark a record whose interval has more than one rank "within flow noise". Pareto filtering on the sky130 plane is done on band-rounded values (two records inside the band do not dominate each other).
4. **Cross-flow statements carry the flow's name.** "A1' ranks the array multiplier last" is a statement about one script; the board never compares a number from one flow with another's.
5. **Do not rank by the mapped (A1') numbers** until a mapper policy is chosen (section 11): their between-script spread (median 12% area, 49% delay) is larger than the bands.
6. **Energy views use the geometric mean of the five official seeds** and the band of 3%; ties inside the band are shown as intervals as above.

## 7. Energy (V = 1.8 V; `spike/sky_energy.py`, `sky_energy_report.py`; fJ per operation)

Definitions (both recorded): **activity-only** `E_act` = zero-delay toggle counts over 2000 consecutive vector transitions of the pinned stimulus: sum over nets of toggles x 1/2 C V^2 (C = fan-out pin capacitances, + 10 fF on every output bit, primary-input nets included) + sum over cell outputs of rises x mean(rise_power tables) + falls x mean(fall_power tables)
at (worst input transition, load), pJ -> fJ; **glitch-aware** `E_glitch` = the same sums over the toggle counts of an inertial-delay event simulation of the first 300 vector pairs (rise/fall cell delays from the STA; every settled value asserted equal to the zero-delay value, which held in all 168 runs). Leakage is separate and negligible (< 0.03 fJ).
Stimulus: vector k of seed s = SHA-256 of `"svgolf-ppa-spike/<s>/<k>"`, uniform over the input bits.

### 7.2 Stimulus protocol (proposal)

* **Public development seed** `0`: 201 vectors (200 transitions), glitch-aware over the first 100 pairs; the contestant's `score` command prints it. Public, fixed, cheap (0.02 s activity; 0.02 to 0.7 s glitch).
* **Official seeds**: five seeds derived as `SHA-256(secret_epoch || task || rtlil_sha256 || i)`, `i = 1..5`, 2001 vectors and 300 timed pairs each (A0': 0.3 s for the adders up to 10 s for the multipliers for all five seeds); **the secret is committed before the record is accepted (`sha256(secret_epoch)` in the record) and published after the record is frozen**, so anyone can recompute; aggregate = **geometric mean of the five energies**, recorded with the five values. Hash of the stimulus specification in the flow pins.
* **Defences**: a combinational design cannot remember vectors, so it can only specialize by comparators on the stimulus (area it pays for); the hidden seeds make that impractical, the geometric mean makes a single lucky vector irrelevant.
* **Which energy defines what**: `energy_glitch` defines the *energy view* and the *energy x delay preset*; `energy_activity` is recorded beside it; **neither defines the default board**.

## 9. Post-synthesis tie: simulating the scored netlist

`check/postsynth.py` simulates the scored netlist in Verilator with `simcells.v` (Yosys's models of its own gate cells: 3758 lines, 90 513 bytes) under `-Wall` with every warning fatal except `DECLFILENAME` and `UNUSEDSIGNAL`. **For the legacy flow and for A0' nothing changes**: the scored netlist is the generic-gate netlist and the sky130 pricing is computed from that very file (record its sha256).
For a sky130-**mapped** netlist (A1') the cell models are needed. Tried for real with the checker's own `vl_build`/`vl_run`/harness (imported read-only by `spike/sky_postsynth_trial.py`; nothing in `check/` was changed or run as `check.py`), 2002 vectors (all-0, all-1, 2000 random):

| netlist | flavour | glue lines | model files | Verilator build (verilate + make, loaded machine) | run | mismatches vs function / vs the study's simulator |
|---|---|--:|--:|--:|--:|--:|
| mul_tree_h N = 8 (A1'u, 221 cells, 24 cell types) | **gen** (liberty-generated) | 121 | 1 | 3.9 s | 0.34 s | 0 / 0 |
| same | **up** (upstream functional + wrappers) | 121 | 25 | 5.8 s | 0.24 s | 0 / 0 |
| mul_array N = 8 (A1's) | gen / up | 151 | 1 / 26 | 4.8 s / 5.4 s | 0.22 / 0.24 s | 0 / 0 |
| sbox_bp (A1'u; contains `mux2_1`, `mux2i_1`, UDP-based) | gen / up | 166 | 1 / 34 | 5.2 s / 3.7 s | 0.11 / 0.18 s | 0 / 0 |
| sbox_table (A1'u; `mux4_2`, `mux2i`) | gen / up | 336 | 1 / 52 | 3.1 s / 3.0 s | 0.08 / 0.17 s | 0 / 0 |

* **gen** = `sky130_cellmodels.generated_models`: one `module <cell>(ports); assign Y = <liberty function>; endmodule` per used cell, 121 to 336 lines for the used cells and **1341 lines (35 353 bytes) for all 268 allowed cells**, from the same liberty the scorer reads; builds under the checker's **unchanged strict lint** (no waivers). No power pins, no specify block, no UDP, no timescale.
* **up** = the pinned upstream `*.functional.v` (85 family files + 3 UDP files, 127 011 bytes, built from Verilog primitives `nand`/`buf` and **UDPs**: `udp_mux_2to1`, `udp_mux_2to1_n`, `udp_mux_4to2`) plus generated one-line drive-strength wrappers (1341 lines for all cells; the family modules are named without the `_N` suffix and
  have no power pins; the upstream `<cell>_N.v` wrappers `include` the family file `<family>.v`, which selects the behavioral/functional/specify variant by defines, and define the module with power pins under `USE_POWER_PINS`: not used here). **Verilator 5.042 accepts the UDPs.** Under the checker's lint it needs: two waivers (`EOFNEWLINE`, the files have no final newline; `TIMESCALEMOD`, the models say `timescale 1ns/1ps` and the netlist does not) and `-I` for the `include "../../models/<udp>/..."` lines (Verilator resolves them against its include path, not the including file). No `specify` blocks are in the functional files (they are in separate `*.specify.v` files, not fetched).
* **Re-reading the netlist** (`reread_check`): `read_verilog -lib <cell models>; hierarchy -top; stat -json` counts cells by liberty cell name (221 instances, 24 types, equal to the scorer's histogram) and `ltp -noff` reports `length=14`, **equal to the engine's cell-level unit depth** on that netlist; so the checker's "fresh Yosys re-reads the netlist and counts the scored gates and the depth" step carries over with `-lib` models.
* **x / z and tie-cell behaviour that differs from the abstract-gate flow.** (a) Verilator is 2-state in both flows; the UDP/primitive models are 4-state in a 4-state simulator (a UDP mux is X-pessimistic only where its table says so) but with known inputs and no unknown constants nothing can become x in either flow. (b) **Constants**: the abstract netlist holds literals (`2'h2`), known 0/1, accepted as today; a mapped netlist with `hilomap` instantiates `conb_1` with **one output connected**, which the checker's lint **rejects (`PINMISSING`)**; with the upstream `conb` model (`pullup`/`pulldown`) it also reports `UNDRIVEN`. Measured on a 6-bit toy design with four constant bits. Hence the tie policy of section 4.2 (literals in the netlist, one priced `conb_1`).
  (c) A **mapped** netlist contains compound cells whose functions the checker has never seen as separate gates: they are simulated from the models, so the netlist-versus-ls-sv comparison is as strong as before, and the models themselves become trusted code (the liberty-generated flavour makes the model and the delay table come from one file).
* **What A1' would force in `check/`**: model generation or the manifest (a new trusted input, hashed), the re-read with `-lib`, lint handling of tie cells, the sky130 netlist hash in the record, and the integrity list of `check.py` (it watches `score.py`, `banlist.py`, `srcgate.py`, `fourstate.py`, `canon.ys`, `svgolf.ys`, `ge.json`) gaining the new scorer files. A0' forces only the last item.

## 12. Decisions for the maintainers

1. **Additive default (recommended) or switch of the default board?** Additive: the legacy (area_ge_v1, depth_v1) board and all 3 records / 45 golden scores stay; sky130 fields are new and versioned. The later decision to make sky130 um^2 and ps the *default* board (and to reprice GE) is yours and forces section 11.1's list.
2. **Which flow produces the official sky130 fields: A0' (priced scored netlist, recommended first) or A1' (real mapping)?** Evidence: A0' preserves every ranking (tau +0.90 to +1.00), needs no new synthesis, ties exactly to the netlist postsynth simulates, is bit-exact and costs 0.05 s; A1' is realistic (compound cells, sizing) but mapper-dominated (the array multiplier moves from 2nd to last; between-script spread 12% area / 49% delay), needs cell models and a tie rule in the checker, and costs a second Yosys run. 3. **Tie bands and the presentation** (10% area, 20% delay, 3% energy, rank intervals): agree, or choose tighter/looser? (Tighter bands rank on mapper noise; looser bands tie the proved trees.)
4. **Constants**: literal in the netlist and one priced `conb_1` per module (recommended) or per-bit `hilomap` cells?
5. **Timing setup**: PI 50 ps, PO 10 fF, no wires, agree? (Rankings are insensitive: tau >= 0.9 over 10 to 300 ps and 5 to 40 fF.)

## 13. Limits, reproduction, files

**Limits.** One library, one corner, no wires (a 130 nm design is more wire-sensitive than the Nangate45 45 nm one: Kogge-Stone's and the trees' wires are not charged; the physical flow is the only one that sees them). N = 16 for add and mul (the scoring point), N = 8 only for the post-synthesis trial; no N sweep in this study. The STA is validated against ABC's STA, not OpenSTA. Energy uses uniform random inputs and the averaged `when`-tables. The
A1' scripts are Yosys 0.53's defaults written out: other Yosys/ABC builds were not tested. The noise-band tables rest on 14 designs (7 with rewrite variants). The tie-cell and constant policy was tested on a toy design only. Cross-machine determinism was not tested.
