## 1. Surprises and findings (all cost at least one compile)

* **`sv_interface` does not read a task that is an application.** The statement of a two-parameter task is
  `(task W P).StatementFixed (sem W P)`, and the tactic unfolds the task only when the first argument of `Interface` is a
  constant; on `task W P` it fails with `internal exception #5` after `the declared ports do not match`. Remedy, one line and
  measured: `unfold SvGolf.Tasks.FdivP.task` before `sv_interface`. (The `CombTask2` patch of `Task.lean.patch` would make the
  library read it; not needed.)
* **Five residual `cover` goals are not closed by `sv_cover` as they stand**: the family covers (`cover_46..49`, `cover_51`
  of the `sv_equations` output) are conjunctions `(row cover) /\ forall k, (cell cover)` (three conjuncts for the `div` rows).
  `refine <?_, ?_> <;> sv_cover` (resp. three) closes them (MEASURED on the goals as standalone examples);
  the 69 other residual goals (`inRange`, `width` with their `max`/`if`,
  `bitRead`, `cover`) are closed by `intros; omega`, `split <;> omega` and `sv_cover`.
* **The equations are not retyped.** `norm_rows`, `div_rows`, the per-shape lemmas take the printed equation as hypothesis,
  with the Int-typed widths and offsets abstracted to `Nat` variables whose values are side conditions (`omega` at the
  use site, after unification reads them off the equation). The one text that must match exactly is the Boolean shape of
  `c_nan` (copied from the print); everything else unifies. A context-width conditional (`max n 32 < w`, from the unsized
  constants) is always decided by `omega` except in `ya_r[0]`/`yb_r[0]`, where both branches have the same value.
* `omega` does not relate `(2:Int)^w` to `((2^w : Nat) : Int)` (two different atoms): `Int` powers go through
  `exact_mod_cast`, or the lemma is stated over `Nat` (`val_constBit1`).
* `rw` closes goals by `rfl` up to reducible unfolding: `rw [h]; rfl` errors with `no goals` when the sides are `a >= b` and
  `b <= a`; `simp only [hb]` with `hb : not (b = true)` rewrites to `false = true`, not to `False` (add `Bool.false_eq_true`).
* The unfold of the printed normalizer row (`ya_r`, `na_r` in context width `max w (max w 32)`) is the only place the
  parametric design differs from fdiv16's flat one: a decrement of an `EW`-bit register inside a wider context is
  `(y + (2^M - 1)) mod 2^w = (y + (2^w - 1)) mod 2^w` (`mod_wrap`).
